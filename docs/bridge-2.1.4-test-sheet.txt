What is new since 2.1.3
  Immediately before each voucher goes to Tally (first post, Retry, Post again; from this browser, another tab,
  another computer, or FinCom's cloud queue), the bridge reads Tally live for that one date and party. A voucher
  already there with the same party ledger, the same bill / reference number, the same date and the same amount is
  NOT posted again: "Already in Tally (voucher no. X, dd-mm-yyyy)".
  If Tally does not answer that check (busy, a message box, closed, too slow, a bad answer), nothing is posted:
  "Could not check Tally, not posted. Try again."
  Ledgers (masters) are not checked this way; they keep "Altered in Tally: it existed already".

Use ZZ TEST only for every posting below, never GARG SHEKHAR & COMPANY.

1. INSTALL OVER 2.1.3
   Do: TallyPrime open with ZZ TEST. Open the Link, then in PowerShell:
       Get-FileHash $env:USERPROFILE\Downloads\FinComBridge-Setup-2.1.4.exe
       Double-click the setup; "Windows protected your PC" > More info > Run anyway; "Just for me"; Next.
   You should see: the Fingerprint above. "FinCom Bridge 2.1.4 is installed and running", "Main bridge: reading and
       posting". You are not asked to pair again. Right-click the icon > Status shows version 2.1.4.
   If not: the page's title and reason; keep "Send install log to FinCom" ticked and click Finish.

2. POSTING ONE ZZ TEST BILL
   Do: in FinCom (staging), open ZZ TEST > Post to Tally. Pick one approved bill (write down its party, bill number,
       date and amount) and press Post 1 to Tally.
   You should see: "In Tally (verified)" with Tally's voucher number. In TallyPrime, Day Book of that date: the
       voucher once. Show log: "voucher ...: sent to Tally; reading it back" and "Import into 'ZZ TEST': 1 of 1
       created". No "NOT POSTED" line.
   If not: what FinCom showed and the log lines of that minute.

3. THE SAME BILL AGAIN FROM A SECOND BROWSER TAB (stale data)
   Do: BEFORE step 2, open ZZ TEST > Post to Tally in a second tab and leave it alone. After step 2, go to that
       second tab (do not reload it): the same bill still shows as not posted. Press Post 1 to Tally there.
   You should see: the bill is refused: "Already in Tally (voucher no. X, dd-mm-yyyy)", X being the number from
       step 2. In TallyPrime, Day Book of that date: still ONE voucher. Show log, one line:
       "duplicate check TDSDesk:... (party, bill, date, amount): NOT POSTED, already in Tally as Journal no. X of
       dd-mm-yyyy (GUID ...)". No "Import into 'ZZ TEST': 1 of 1 created" for it.
   Then: in the first tab, press Post again (or Retry) on the same bill: refused the same way.
   If not: a screenshot of both tabs, the Day Book of that date, and the log of those minutes.

4. POSTING WHILE TALLY SHOWS A MESSAGE BOX
   Do: in TallyPrime press Alt+F4 so the "Quit?" box shows, and leave it open. In FinCom, post another approved ZZ
       TEST bill (one not in Tally yet). Wait 1 minute.
   You should see one of these, and never "In Tally":
       "Could not check Tally, not posted. Try again." (the check was not answered within 25 s), or
       "Waiting for Tally: Tally is busy ..." (Tally did not answer before the check was reached; nothing sent).
       Show log: "duplicate check ...: NOT POSTED, could not check Tally: Tally is busy and did not answer in time
       ..." (or the waiting line). In TallyPrime, after closing the box with No: the bill is NOT in the Day Book
       (unless it was "Waiting" and went in by itself after you closed the box: then once only).
   Then: close the box (No). Press Try again / Retry in FinCom: it posts, "In Tally (verified)", once.
   If not: what FinCom showed, the Day Book of that date, and the log of those minutes.

5. NORMAL POSTING STILL WORKS
   Do: post two more approved ZZ TEST bills together (Post 2 to Tally), one of them for the same party and date as
       step 2 but a different bill number.
   You should see: both "In Tally (verified)", each once in the Day Book. No "NOT POSTED" line in the log. The
       posting takes about as long as it did on 2.1.3 (a second or two more at most).
   If not: what FinCom showed and the log of those minutes.

6. CLEAN UP
   Do: in TallyPrime delete the ZZ TEST vouchers made in steps 2, 4 and 5 (or use Remove from Tally in FinCom).
   Then (optional): post the bill of step 2 again. It goes in (it was removed, so the check does not find it).

IF SOMETHING FAILS, SEND ME

   c) icon > Show log, the part from the start;
   d) icon > Status (the text).
