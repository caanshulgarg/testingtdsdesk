What is new since 2.1.2
  a) The bridge no longer polls Tally. It sends Tally nothing until one of: a client opened in FinCom, Update now,
     a posting, or the nightly catch-up (02:00, only when Tally is open and nobody has used FinCom for 15 minutes).
  b) A posting always wins: a background read is stopped at once, the posting goes, and the read resumes from where
     it was. No more "Tally (port 9002) is busy with another FinCom request".
  c) No request to Tally runs longer than 25 s. Opening balances are read in batches of ledgers, each batch saved.
     After a failure the next batch is half the size, and Tally is left alone (one log line) for a while.
  d) Tray: "Pause background reading" (postings and Update now still work) and "Nightly catch-up at 02:00".
  e) FinCom shows each client's Tally in one line with Update now, and "Books as of 15:34 · Update now".
     This needs the app and tally-ingest published on staging. Until then, steps 3, 9 and 10 only show what the
     tray and the log say.

Why by hand: 2.1.1/2.1.2 take an update only from a signed update list. The 2.1.3 list is not signed (that is yours).

1. INSTALL OVER 2.1.1
   Do: TallyPrime open with ZZ TEST and GARG SHEKHAR & COMPANY. Open the Link, then in PowerShell:
       Get-FileHash $env:USERPROFILE\Downloads\FinComBridge-Setup-2.1.3.exe
       Double-click the setup; "Windows protected your PC" > More info > Run anyway; "Just for me"; Next.
   You should see: the Fingerprint above. "FinCom Bridge 2.1.3 is installed and running", "Main bridge: reading and
       posting". You are not asked to pair again. Show log (right-click the icon) has:
       "Tally is open on port 9002; the bridge reads it only after an event (...)".
       There is no "Waiting for Tally to answer promptly" and no company list at the start.
   If not: the page's title and reason; keep "Send install log to FinCom" ticked and click Finish.

2. IDLE FOR 30 MINUTES: NOTHING SENT TO TALLY
   Do: right-click the icon > Status. Write down "Requests sent to Tally since the bridge started: N".
       Leave FinCom closed (or on the Home page, no client open) and do not post. Work in TallyPrime as usual for
       30 minutes. Then open Status again, and Show log.
   You should see: the same N. In Show log, no new lines in those 30 minutes except, at most, "Heartbeat" lines.
       No TDSDeskCompanies, no "Light check", no "Keeping ... in step", no "reading every opening balance".
       Tally answers at once the whole time.
   If not: both Status texts and the log lines of those 30 minutes.

3. OPENING A CLIENT: ONE LIGHT UPDATE
   Do: in FinCom (staging), open the client ZZ TEST. Wait 1 minute. Then go Home and open ZZ TEST again, twice.
   You should see in Show log, once only:
       "Woken by FinCom ..." or nothing, then
       "Light update of ZZ TEST (opened in FinCom): only what changed in Tally since the last read" and
       "Light update: done (3 request(s) to Tally); the bridge is idle again" (a small number, not hundreds).
       Opening it again within 5 minutes adds no line.
   If not: the log lines from the first opening. (Before tally-ingest is published, this works only with FinCom
       open on NWS144 itself.)

4. UPDATE NOW
   Do: ZZ TEST > Post to Tally (or the Tally page), press "Update now" next to the Tally line.
   You should see in Show log: "Woken by FinCom: Update now" (from another computer) or "Update from Tally asked for
       now", then "Update from Tally: asked for now" ... "Update from Tally: done (N request(s) to Tally); the
       bridge is idle again (next: the nightly catch-up at 02:00, or Update now)".
       In FinCom the line shows "last read" with the time of this update.
   If not: the log from the press to 5 minutes later.

5. A POSTING TO ZZ TEST DURING A READ (ZZ TEST only, never GARG SHEKHAR & COMPANY)
   Do: press Update now (step 4) and, while Status says "Update from Tally: running now", post one approved bill of
       ZZ TEST (Post to Tally > Post 1 to Tally).
   You should see: the posting goes within a few seconds: "In Tally (verified)". Show log:
       "Tally 9002: a background read was stopped at once so FinCom's request goes first; it resumes afterwards
       from where it was", "Keeping ZZ TEST: paused for FinCom's request (a posting goes first); ...", the posting's
       lines, then the update carries on and ends with "Update from Tally: done".
       Never "busy with another FinCom request".
   If not: the time you pressed Post, what FinCom showed, and the log of those minutes.

6. PAUSE BACKGROUND READING
   Do: right-click the icon > "Pause background reading". Open the client GARG SHEKHAR & COMPANY in FinCom. Then
       press Update now for ZZ TEST.
   You should see: the menu now says "Resume background reading"; the tooltip ends "Background reading paused".
       Show log: "Background reading paused from the tray icon: ...". Opening the client adds no "Light update"

       In FinCom: "Background reading paused on NWS144".

7. CLOSING TALLY: "Tally is closed"
   Do: close TallyPrime. Wait 1 minute. Look at the tray tooltip, Status, and in FinCom ZZ TEST > Post to Tally.
   You should see: tooltip "... - Tally not open"; Status "Tally: not open in your Windows session"; FinCom

       its port). Open TallyPrime again: within 1 minute FinCom says "Tally open on NWS144 · last read ...".
   If not: what each place said, and the log of those minutes.

8. THE NIGHTLY CATCH-UP SETTING
   Do: right-click the icon > the menu shows "Nightly catch-up at 02:00" (greyed). Status says "... the nightly
       catch-up at 02:00 (only when Tally is open and nobody has used FinCom for 15 minutes; KeepDailyAt in the
       settings)". To try it now: in %LOCALAPPDATA%\TDS Desk Bridge\tds-bridge.config.json set
       "KeepDailyAt": "HH:MM" to 20 minutes from now and save; leave FinCom unused, Tally open, do not post.
   You should see: the menu shows the new time within a minute. At that time (or 15 minutes after FinCom was last
       used): "Nightly catch-up (HH:MM): Tally is open and nobody has used FinCom for 15 minutes" ... "Nightly
       catch-up: done (...)". If FinCom was used meanwhile: "Nightly catch-up waits: FinCom was used at ..."
       (said once). Set it back to "02:00" afterwards.
   If not: the setting you saved and the log from that time.
