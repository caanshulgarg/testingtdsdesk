FINCOM BRIDGE 2.2.1 - TEST SHEET (NWS144, staging)

File:        FinComBridge-Setup-2.2.1.exe
Fingerprint: (filled in when the setup is built; compare with the .sha256 file next to the setup)
Link:        https://staging.fincom.live/review/assets/bridge-go/FinComBridge-Setup-2.2.1.exe

What 2.2.1 changes: a NEW entry you save in Tally now reaches FinCom as Created, with Tally's own GUID and the whole
entry, and goes into the books. (2.2.0 sent it as Altered, without the entry.) Receipts 191 and 192 of 05-Oct-2026,
which 2.2.0 sent that way, are sent again by 2.2.1 by itself as Created; you do not enter them again.

Keep the add-on loaded as it is (C:\ProgramData\FinCom\addon\FinComRecorder.tdl). 2.2.1 does not change the add-on.

In every entry you make for this sheet, start the narration with TRIAL.

Save time: TDL gives the time to the minute only; save time is measured by the owner with a stopwatch. FinCom shows
Tally's minute as the save time, and the second the bridge read the line as the time received.


INSTALL (over 2.2.0)
   Open the Link. Double-click the setup; if Windows warns: More info > Run anyway; "Just for me" (as before); Next.
   You should see: "FinCom Bridge 2.2.1 is installed and running". The setup keeps 2.2.0, so you can go back to it
   (HOW TO ROLL BACK, below).
   Within a minute, FinCom's Tally page shows version 2.2.1 for this computer.


CHECK 1 - RECEIPTS 191 AND 192 (no action in Tally)
   Wait two minutes after installing.
   You should see: FinCom > Tally page > Sync activity shows Receipts 191 and 192 (05-Oct-2026) as Created, and both
   are in the books (Look up and the ledger show them). You did not enter them again. They show once each.

CHECK 2 - A NEW ENTRY ARRIVES AS CREATED AND ENTERS THE BOOKS
   In Tally make one new entry (for example a Receipt of Rs 1, narration "TRIAL new 1") and save it. Note its number.
   You should see: within half a minute, Sync activity shows it as Created (not Altered), and it is in the books.

CHECK 3 - ALTER IT: CHANGE THE AMOUNT
   Open the entry from CHECK 2 in Tally, change its amount to Rs 2 and save.
   You should see: within half a minute, Sync activity shows it as Altered, with the new amount (Rs 2).

CHECK 4 - DELETE ONE
   Open another TRIAL entry in Tally and delete it (Alt+D, Yes).
   You should see: within half a minute, Sync activity shows it as Deleted.

CHECK 5 - CANCEL ONE
   Open another TRIAL entry in Tally and cancel it (Alt+X, Yes).
   You should see: within half a minute, Sync activity shows it as Cancelled.

CHECK 6 - LOOK UP AND THE LEDGER
   In FinCom open Look up and the ledger of the party of CHECK 2.
   You should see: the new entry of CHECK 2 is there, with its changed amount (Rs 2) from CHECK 3; the deleted entry
   of CHECK 4 is gone; the cancelled entry of CHECK 5 shows as cancelled.

If any check does not show what it says: note the check, the entry's type and number and the time, and tell FinCom.
The bridge's log (right-click the FinCom icon > Show log) says why a line went without its entry.


HOW TO ROLL BACK (go back to 2.2.0)
   1. Right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > "Roll back to the previous version".
   2. A question names the version it goes back to (2.2.0). Answer Yes.
   3. The icon goes away for a few seconds and comes back.
   You should see: within a minute, FinCom's Tally page shows version 2.2.0 for this computer. Automatic updates stay
   off on this computer until FinCom turns them on again.
