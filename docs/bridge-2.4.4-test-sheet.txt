FINCOM 2.4.4 - TEST SHEET (NWS144, staging)

File:        FinComBridge-Setup-2.4.4.exe
Fingerprint: (filled at build; compare with the .sha256 file next to the setup)

What 2.4.4 changes (the full notes: docs/bridge-2.4.4-notes.md):
1. Payroll from Tally: the employees (name, PAN, group, designation, date of joining and leaving), the pay heads, each
   employee's amount per pay head, and the salary paid to each employee reach FinCom. Nothing personal: no date of birth,
   Aadhaar, bank account or IFSC is read or kept.
2. Under the client's TDS, a Salary tab: Employees (type in the regime and declarations Tally does not keep), Pay
   register, Form 16. The 24Q return of a quarter is filled from Tally's payroll: Annexure I (with each month's challan),
   Annexure II in Q4, and the text file, marked "check before filing" (run it through the FVU first).
3. The bridge asks Tally for the employees only when masters change, and the ledger list asks four pay-head fields. A
   payroll entry may take up to 3 seconds; everything else keeps 2 seconds. One request at a time.
   A payroll voucher type of your own (not Tally's "Payroll") gets the 3 seconds after its first entry was read.
   Bank account numbers, IFSC and the name in favour are taken out of every entry the bridge keeps or sends.
4. FinCom's cloud stores these (migration 74, run on staging before the tally-ingest of 2.4.4).
The add-on is 2.4.3's.

WHAT YOUR STAFF DO IN TALLY (so payroll reaches FinCom)
   a. Payroll on: F11 (Features) > Maintain payroll: Yes.
   b. Employees: Payroll Masters > Employee Groups, then Employees: the name, the group, designation, date of joining, and
      PAN (Statutory details). Date of leaving when someone leaves. (Bank and Aadhaar may be kept in Tally; FinCom never
      reads them.)
   c. Pay heads: each with its pay head type, and for earnings the Income Tax component (Basic Salary, House Rent
      Allowance...); the TDS on salary as a pay head of type Employees' Statutory Deductions, statutory type Income Tax.
   d. Each month: a Payroll voucher (Ctrl+F4) with Payroll Autofill for the employees; the salary paid with a Payment voucher
      (Payroll Autofill > Salary Payment, so each employee's amount is on the bank line); the TDS paid with Stat Payment of
      Income Tax, then Challan Reconciliation (BSR code, challan number, date) as for other TDS since 2.4.3.

Before you start: migration 74 has run on staging and the tally-ingest of 2.4.4 is deployed; you are signed in to FinCom as
the owner; TallyPrime 7.1 is open on NWS144 with a ZZ TEST company. In every entry you make for this sheet, start the
narration with TRIAL. Write down the time (IST) of each check.

CHECK 1 - THE VERSION
   Install FinComBridge-Setup-2.4.4.exe and compare its fingerprint. The add-on is unchanged: nothing to reload.
   You should see: the tray says FinCom Bridge 2.4.4; FinCom's Tally page shows version 2.4.4.

CHECK 2 - EMPLOYEES
   In the ZZ TEST company, do a. to c. above for two employees (one with PAN, one without). Wait a minute (or Update now).
   You should see: TDS > Salary > Employees lists both, the one without PAN marked "no PAN"; no other cost centre listed.

CHECK 3 - PAYROLL
   Do d. for one month (narration "TRIAL 244 payroll"; salary payment "TRIAL 244 salary").
   You should see: the Pay register of that month with each pay head per employee; the log shows the payroll entry
   answered in under 3 seconds.

CHECK 4 - 24Q
   Choose the regime for each employee under Employees. Open TDS > Returns > 24Q for that quarter.
   You should see: Annexure I with each employee's salary and TDS for the month, the challan beside it; Files: the text
   file says "Check before filing".

CHECK 5 - NOTHING SLOWER
   Save an ordinary Journal of Rs 1, narration "TRIAL 244 speed".
   You should see: it is in FinCom's books as fast as with 2.4.3 (a few seconds).

How to send the log lines: right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > Show log.

HOW TO ROLL BACK (go back to 2.4.3)
   1. Right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > "Roll back to the previous version".
   2. A question names the version it goes back to (2.4.3). Answer Yes.
   3. The icon goes away for a few seconds and comes back.
   You should see: within a minute, FinCom's Tally page shows version 2.4.3 for your line. 2.4.3 sends no employees and no
   pay-head fields; what 2.4.4 sent stays. Automatic updates stay off on this computer until FinCom turns them on again.
