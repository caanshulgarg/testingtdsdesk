FINCOM BRIDGE 2.2.0 - TEST SHEET (NWS144, staging)

File:        FinComBridge-Setup-2.2.0.exe
Fingerprint: SHA-256 (filled in when the setup is built; compare with the .sha256 file next to the setup)
Link:        https://staging.fincom.live/review/assets/bridge-go/FinComBridge-Setup-2.2.0.exe
(2.1.10 is installed on NWS144. Install 2.2.0 over it; the installer replaces the program and keeps the pairing, the
 settings and the copy. 2.2.0 is published only after the owner's recorder trial results are in.)

In this sheet, "the company you test on" is the one company you choose: it must be linked to a FinCom client. The
sheet names no company.

What is new in 2.2.0 (over 2.1.10)
  - The live recorder. A new add-on, C:\ProgramData\FinCom\addon\FinComRecorder.tdl, written by the install next to
    the trial's FinComRecorderAnyCompany.tdl (which is not changed). While it is loaded in Tally it appends one short
    line per save, alteration, deletion, cancellation or import, for every company open, to
    C:\ProgramData\FinCom\recorder\<company GUID>-<yyyymmdd>.txt. Nothing else happens inside Tally.
      - The bridge reads the newest 7 days of those files once a second (never writing, locking, renaming or deleting
        them), turns the lines into changes (created, altered, deleted, cancelled, imported; ledger created, altered,
        deleted), and sends them to FinCom in groups of at most 500 lines. A line counts as sent only when FinCom
        answers that it took it; otherwise it is sent again later. After a restart nothing is sent twice.
      - For an entry someone typed in Tally (not FinCom's own posting), the bridge asks Tally for that one entry by its
        Tally id, on its own date (a new, small request: FinComVoucherByMaster, at most 50 entries at a time). For a
        ledger created or altered, that one ledger. A posting always goes first: these requests wait for it, and give
        up after 20 s (the line then goes without the entry's body; FinCom holds it until the Day Book brings it).
      - While a posting runs, the lines go only between Tally's import requests, at most one group each time; the
        posting never waits for them.
      - FinCom can choose per computer where the changes come from: the add-on (the default), Tally's change list (no
        add-on: the bridge asks Tally for the entries changed since the last answer, every 10 minutes or so), or both.
        Tally's change list never runs unless the owner chooses it for this computer. It asks Tally at most once a
        minute and never during a posting; if Tally takes more than 2 s for one answer, it turns itself off for that
        company (the log says "Source B off: Tally took X.X s for the changed-entries list (limit 2 s)"; it stays off
        after a restart) until the owner chooses the source again on FinCom's Tally page.
  - The posting window: before each posting the company check notes Tally's change number (ALTVCHID); after the last
    import one more company check notes it again; the two numbers and how many entries and masters Tally created go
    with the job's last report, so FinCom's gap check does not count FinCom's own postings as missing.
  - "Roll back to the previous version" in the tray (always there; it asks yes/no first, naming the version). From
    2.2.0 on, the version an update replaced is kept; the rollback puts it back, starts it, and turns automatic
    updates off on this computer until FinCom support turns them on again.
  - The bridge's log is kept 30 days: each day's log is renamed to its date the next day (tds-bridge.log.yyyy-mm-dd);
    copies older than 30 days are deleted (only the bridge's own log copies).
  - The heartbeat tells FinCom, per company, how many recorder lines were read, sent and are waiting, the oldest
    waiting, and where the changes come from.
  - The trial tools and the trial add-on are unchanged. Posting is unchanged.

0. BEFORE THE INSTALL
   The recorder trial is done (docs/recorder-trial-sheet.txt) and its add-on unloaded: in TallyPrime F1 (Help) >
   TDLs & Add-Ons > F4 (Manage Local TDLs): no line with FinComRecorderAnyCompany.tdl or FinComRecorderTrial.tdl.
   TallyPrime open with the company you test on.

1. DOWNLOAD AND INSTALL OVER 2.1.10
   Do: open the Link, check the fingerprint if you can (PowerShell: Get-FileHash <file>). Double-click the setup;
       "Windows protected your PC" > More info > Run anyway; "Just for me" (as before); Next.
   You should see: "FinCom Bridge 2.2.0 is installed and running", "Main bridge: reading and posting".
   Check: the file C:\ProgramData\FinCom\addon\FinComRecorder.tdl exists (File Explorer, only look).
   Check: right-click the FinCom icon near the clock: "Roll back to the previous version" is in the menu.

2. LOAD THE LIVE ADD-ON
   Do: PART 1 of docs/recorder-live-sheet.txt (load C:\ProgramData\FinCom\addon\FinComRecorder.tdl; never together with
       the trial add-on).
   You should see: on TDLs & Add-Ons the file shows as Loaded.

3. A CHANGE TYPED IN TALLY
   Do: in the company you test on, make a Payment of Rs 1 (narration "TRIAL live 1"), save it. Then open it, change the
       narration to "TRIAL live 1 changed", save. Then cancel it (Alt+X, Yes).
   You should see: within a minute, the client in FinCom shows the entry made, changed and cancelled, without a Day
       Book upload. In "Show log" no line starting "Recorder:" says a body was not read.
   Note: how long each save took, compared with before the add-on was loaded (write it down if it felt slower).

4. A POSTING WHILE LINES ARE WRITTEN
   Do: post 20 bills from FinCom to the client linked to the company you test on (the add-on stays loaded).
   You should see: "Posted 20 of 20" as quickly as with 2.1.10. Within a minute after it, the client shows the 20
       entries once each (not twice), and FinCom's Tally page shows no "changes not received" for them.

5. A RESTART
   Do: tray > Restart. While it restarts, save one more Payment of Rs 1 in Tally ("TRIAL live 2").
   You should see: after the restart that entry reaches FinCom once; nothing from before is sent again.

6. ROLL BACK (only if the owner asks for it)
   Do: tray > "Roll back to the previous version". A yes/no names the version it goes back to (2.1.10). Answer No
       to leave it; Yes only when asked to roll back.
   On a computer that was installed fresh with 2.2.0 it says "No previous version is kept on this computer".

7. UNLOAD (to switch the live add-on off)
   PART 2 of docs/recorder-live-sheet.txt. The bridge still sends the lines already written.

If anything is not as written: tray > "Send install log to FinCom", and note the step number.
