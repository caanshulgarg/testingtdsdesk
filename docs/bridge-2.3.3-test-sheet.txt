What 2.3.3 changes (the full notes: docs/bridge-2.3.3-notes.md). The owner's rule: "A save must always show on the
Tally page, at least as held with a reason. Silence is not acceptable."
1. An entry whose details Tally does not give on the first attempt goes up to FinCom at once, held, with words such as
   "waiting: Tally took longer than 2 s; FinCom asks again at 12:15". Its details follow later (the held line is then
   replaced and the entry enters the books once). No line waits unsent more than a few seconds.
2. A held line is asked of Tally again ONCE (one request). If that ask is not answered in time, or Tally does not give
   the entry, the line ends at once with "... upload that day's Day Book to settle it" and is never asked again. The
   held lines from earlier no longer hold back new saves: the newest are asked first.
   Nothing is sent to Tally while it is still working on an earlier request the bridge stopped waiting for; a posting
   waits for it (10 minutes at most; in practice a second or two), and the Tally page says "Tally is still finishing an
   earlier request".
3. A company slow on every entry (GARG SHEKHAR, about 2.2 s) is now marked "no longer asked" even when only its entries
   are being asked. A Tally that freezes as a whole still never marks a company. Once marked, none of its lines, old or
   new, is asked again: new ones go up held at once, held ones end with the same words.
4. When lines have waited 30 seconds or more, one line on the Tally page says how many and since when.
No Tally request was added or changed. FinCom's cloud and app are unchanged (no migration). The add-on is unchanged.

Before you start: you are signed in to FinCom as the owner, TallyPrime 7.1 is open on NWS144 with the add-on loaded,
GARG SHEKHAR & COMPANY and a small company are open in the Tally of the Windows user whose bridge you check. In every
entry you make for this sheet, start the narration with TRIAL. Write down the time (IST) of each check.

CHECK 1 - THE VERSION
   Install FinComBridge-Setup-2.3.3.exe and compare its fingerprint with the one above.
   You should see: the tray says FinCom Bridge 2.3.3; FinCom's Tally page shows version 2.3.3 for your line.

CHECK 2 - A FAST COMPANY AS BEFORE
   In the small company make a Journal of Rs 1, narration "TRIAL 233 small".
   You should see: within a minute it is in the books with its details. Nothing new on the Tally page.

CHECK 3 - A SAVE IN THE SLOW COMPANY SHOWS AT ONCE
   In GARG SHEKHAR make a Journal of Rs 1, narration "TRIAL 233 slow 1". Open FinCom's Sync activity for it.
   You should see: within a few seconds the line is there, held, "Received, not yet entered in the books: waiting: Tally
   took longer than 2 s; FinCom asks again at <time>" (or "Tally busy"). It is never missing.
   If not, send: the time, a photo of Sync activity, and the Show log lines starting "Recorder:".

CHECK 4 - THE SLOW COMPANY IS MARKED
   Make two more Journals in GARG SHEKHAR ("TRIAL 233 slow 2", "TRIAL 233 slow 3") about 10 minutes apart. Wait 20
   minutes in all.
   You should see: NWS144's card on the Tally page says "GARG SHEKHAR & COMPANY: FinCom has stopped asking Tally for this
   company's entries, because finding one entry took Tally longer than 2 seconds (since <date time>) ...". The log has
   one line "GARG SHEKHAR & COMPANY: entry fetch stopped: over 2 s (...)". Its held lines then end with the Day Book
   words; upload its Day Book to settle them.

CHECK 5 - A HELD LINE IS ASKED AGAIN ONCE
   If a line of the small company shows "waiting: ..." (Tally busy for a moment), wait for the time it names.
   You should see: the line becomes "Replaced by a later line" and the entry is in the books once. If Tally was still
   busy then, the line instead shows "... upload that day's Day Book to settle it"; upload that day's Day Book.
   The lines held before you installed 2.3.3 settle the same way within a few minutes: each is asked once.

CHECK 6 - NOTHING SILENT
   Over the day, every journal you saved shows in Sync activity: in the books, or held with a reason. None is missing.

How to send the log lines: right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > Show log. On NWS144 each Windows user has their
own FinCom icon and their own log: send the log of the Windows user named in the check.

HOW TO ROLL BACK (go back to 2.3.2)
   1. Right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > "Roll back to the previous version".
   2. A question names the version it goes back to (2.3.2). Answer Yes.
   3. The icon goes away for a few seconds and comes back.
   You should see: within a minute, FinCom's Tally page shows version 2.3.2 for your line. Automatic updates stay off on
   this computer until FinCom turns them on again.
