FINCOM 2.3.5 - TEST SHEET (NWS144, staging)

File:        FinComBridge-Setup-2.3.5.exe
Fingerprint: (filled in when the setup is built)

What 2.3.5 changes (the full notes: docs/bridge-2.3.5-notes.md):
1. The Tally pages are simpler: one card per computer with one status line, three steps, the rest under More; Sync
   activity shows "Needs you" only when a person must act.
2. Clear notifications everywhere: in FinCom (bell and page lines, with Undo) and in the bridge's tray. "if one time
   any notification is cleared then that notification should not appear" (the owner).
3. Held lines under FinCom's read stop: a held delete keeps its entry id; the line says reading is stopped; a refused
   request is not a try, a real Tally failure still is.
No Tally request added or changed. FinCom's cloud: migration 68 (the owner runs it). The add-on is unchanged.

Before you start: you are signed in to FinCom as the owner, TallyPrime 7.1 is open on NWS144 with the add-on loaded,
GARG SHEKHAR & COMPANY and IX DESIGNS open in the Tally of the Windows user whose bridge you check. In every entry you
make for this sheet, start the narration with TRIAL. Write down the time (IST) of each check.

CHECK 1 - THE VERSION
   Install FinComBridge-Setup-2.3.5.exe and compare its fingerprint with the one above.
   You should see: the tray says FinCom Bridge 2.3.5; FinCom's Tally page shows version 2.3.5 for your computer (under
   More if it is not on the card).

CHECK 2 - THE TALLY PAGE
   Open FinCom's Tally page.
   You should see: one card for NWS144 and your Windows user with one status line ("Connected · Tally open: ...");
   everything else under More. Close Tally: within a few minutes the line says Tally is not open on NWS144. Open it
   again: the line says Connected.

CHECK 3 - SYNC ACTIVITY
   In GARG SHEKHAR make a Journal of Rs 1, narration "TRIAL 235 sync 1". Open Sync activity.
   You should see: no permanent yellow box; the entry in the books within a few seconds. A yellow "Needs you" appears
   only for lines a person must settle, one sentence per company and day with one button.

CHECK 4 - CLEAR NOTIFICATIONS IN FINCOM
   Open the bell. Clear one item, then reload the page and open FinCom on your phone.
   You should see: the cleared item does not come back, on either. Clear all, then Undo: they are back. On the phone
   the line's words are readable on their own line, the buttons below, nothing scrolls sideways.

CHECK 5 - CLEAR NOTIFICATIONS IN THE TRAY
   Close Tally for 3 minutes: the tray shows "Tally not open" once. Close it with X. Open and close Tally again, and
   restart the icon (right-click > Restart).
   You should see: the same notification is not shown again today. Right-click the icon > Clear notifications: it says
   how many were cleared. The icon's colour and tooltip still show the state.

CHECK 6 - A DELETE WHILE READING IS STOPPED
   In IX DESIGNS make a Journal of Rs 1, narration "TRIAL 235 delete", wait until it is in the books. On FinCom's Tally
   page (More) stop reading from Tally for NWS144, then delete the entry in Tally (Day Book, select it, Alt+D, Yes).
   You should see: Sync activity shows the delete waiting with "reading from Tally is stopped from FinCom; asked again
   when it is resumed". Resume reading: within a minute the entry is gone from FinCom's books, not held.
   If not, send: the time, a photo of Sync activity, and the Show log lines with "delete of mid".

How to send the log lines: right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > Show log. On NWS144 each Windows user has their
own FinCom icon and their own log: send the log of the Windows user named in the check.

HOW TO ROLL BACK (go back to 2.3.4)
   1. Right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > "Roll back to the previous version".
   2. A question names the version it goes back to (2.3.4). Answer Yes.
   3. The icon goes away for a few seconds and comes back.
   You should see: within a minute, FinCom's Tally page shows version 2.3.4 for your line. Automatic updates stay off on
   this computer until FinCom turns them on again.
