What 2.3.1 changes (the full notes: docs/bridge-2.3.1-notes.md): one thing. A Sales, Purchase, Credit Note or Debit
Note entered WITH stock items (item invoice mode) now enters the books complete. Tally keeps the sales or purchase
ledger of such an invoice under its items; 2.3.0 did not ask Tally for it, so FinCom held these entries ("the entry's
details from Tally are incomplete (its lines do not add up ...)"). 2.3.1 also asks Tally for the ledger lines under the
items, so the entry arrives with every line: the party, the sales or purchase ledger, CGST and SGST (or IGST).
Nothing else changed: the same entries per request as before (by MasterID up to 50 entries of one day; by type and
number one entry), read only, inside the 2-second rule. Two limits: a very large invoice (over about 1 MB of details,
roughly 1,000 items or more) is held with plain words for a Day Book upload; the item lines carry no HSN or GST rate from
this path (FinCom's reports read the Day Book upload, so they are unaffected).

The app is not updated with this bridge. Keep the add-on loaded as it is
(C:\ProgramData\FinCom\addon\FinComRecorder.tdl). 2.3.1 does not change the add-on.

Before you start: you are signed in to FinCom as the owner, TallyPrime 7.1 is open with the add-on loaded, and
GARG SHEKHAR is open in Tally. In every entry you make for this sheet, start the narration with TRIAL. Use stock items
and ledgers that already exist in GARG SHEKHAR (any items; a sales ledger and a purchase ledger with GST; the CGST and
SGST ledgers).

Write down the time (IST) of each check you do; we need it if something fails.


INSTALL (over 2.3.0, for your own Windows user)
   New versions install by themselves on every computer. To install it at once: open the Link, double-click the setup;
   if Windows warns: More info > Run anyway; "Just for me" (as before); Next.
   You should see: "FinCom Bridge 2.3.1 is installed and running". Within a minute, FinCom's Tally page shows version
   2.3.1 for your line ("NWS144 · <your Windows user>").
   Every Windows user on NWS144 that runs a FinCom Bridge gets 2.3.1 the same way.


CHECK 1 - A SALES INVOICE WITH TWO ITEMS AND GST
   In Tally make a Sales entry in item invoice mode: a party, TWO stock items (for example 2 of one item and 3 of
   another), the sales ledger with GST, and CGST and SGST on the invoice. Narration "TRIAL 231 sales". Save it. Note its
   number, the invoice total, the taxable value and the CGST and SGST amounts Tally shows.
   You should see: within a minute, Sync activity shows it as Created, and it is in the books (not held). Look up shows
   every line: the party for the invoice total, the sales ledger for the taxable value (the two items together), CGST
   and SGST for Tally's amounts. The lines add up to the total, as in Tally.
   If not, send: its type and number, the time you saved it, a picture of the entry in Tally, what Sync activity shows
   for it, and the Show log lines starting "Recorder:" that name it.

CHECK 2 - A PURCHASE INVOICE WITH ITEMS
   In Tally make a Purchase entry in item invoice mode: a supplier, one or two stock items, the purchase ledger with
   GST, CGST and SGST (input). Narration "TRIAL 231 purchase". Save it. Note its number and Tally's figures.
   You should see: within a minute, Sync activity shows it as Created, and it is in the books with every line: the
   supplier for the total, the purchase ledger for the taxable value, CGST and SGST input for Tally's amounts.
   If not, send: its type and number, the time, a picture of the entry in Tally, and the Show log lines starting
   "Recorder:" that name it.

CHECK 3 - A CREDIT NOTE WITH ITEMS
   In Tally make a Credit Note in item invoice mode against the party of CHECK 1: one of the items returned, the sales
   ledger, CGST and SGST. Narration "TRIAL 231 credit note". Save it. Note its number and Tally's figures.
   You should see: within a minute, Sync activity shows it as Created, and it is in the books with every line: the
   party, the sales ledger, CGST and SGST, matching Tally.
   If not, send: its type and number, the time, a picture of the entry in Tally, and the Show log lines starting
   "Recorder:" that name it.

CHECK 4 - ALTER THE SALES INVOICE
   Open the Sales entry of CHECK 1 in Tally, change the quantity of one item, and save.
   You should see: within a minute, Sync activity shows it as Altered, and the books show Tally's new figures for every
   line (party, sales ledger, CGST, SGST).
   If not, send: its type and number, the time, and the Show log lines starting "Recorder:" that name it.

CHECK 5 - ITEM INVOICES HELD UNDER 2.3.0 SETTLE BY THEMSELVES
   Only if an item invoice was held under 2.3.0 with the words "the entry's details from Tally are incomplete (its
   lines do not add up ...)". Wait up to 15 minutes after installing 2.3.1, with that company open in Tally.
   This includes an item invoice 2.3.0 had already asked Tally for again (held a second time with the same words): it
   is asked once more and settles the same way (needs FinCom's cloud update of 2.3.1).
   You should see: the held entry is in the books with every line, once, and its held lines show as 'replaced'.
   If not, send: its type, number and date, and the Show log lines starting "Recorder:" that name it.

CHECK 6 - EVERYTHING ELSE AS IN 2.3.0
   Make one Receipt of Rs 1, narration "TRIAL 231 receipt". Save it.
   You should see: within a minute, Sync activity shows it as Created, and it is in the books, as with 2.3.0.
   If not, send: its number, the time, and the Show log lines starting "Recorder:" that name it.

How to send the log lines: right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > Show log. Each line starting "Recorder:" says
what the bridge decided for one entry: asking Tally, taken, held (with the reason), or not asked (with the reason).
On NWS144 each Windows user has their own FinCom icon and their own log: send the log of the Windows user named in the
check.


HOW TO ROLL BACK (go back to 2.3.0)
   1. Right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > "Roll back to the previous version".
   2. A question names the version it goes back to (2.3.0). Answer Yes.
   3. The icon goes away for a few seconds and comes back.
   You should see: within a minute, FinCom's Tally page shows version 2.3.0 for your line. The app does not change:
   it trusts 2.3.0 as before. Item invoices are then held again as under 2.3.0 (uploading that day's Day Book settles
   them). Automatic updates stay off on this computer until FinCom turns them on again.
