What 2.3.1 changes (the full notes: docs/bridge-2.3.1-notes.md): one thing. A Sales, Purchase, Credit Note or Debit
Note entered WITH stock items (item invoice mode) now enters the books complete. Tally keeps the sales or purchase
ledger of such an invoice under its items; 2.3.0 did not ask Tally for it, so FinCom held these entries ("the entry's
details from Tally are incomplete (its lines do not add up ...)"). 2.3.1 also asks Tally for the ledger lines under the
items, so the entry arrives with every line: the party, the sales or purchase ledger, CGST and SGST (or IGST).
With it (part A) the whole entry comes: the items (name, quantity, unit, rate, taxable value, the HSN or SAC and GST
rate Tally applied to that line, and the CGST / SGST / IGST / cess per line, worked out from the line's rate as Tally
does: Tally keeps no tax amount per item line), bill-wise details, TDS details, cost centres, bank details (UTR,
instrument date, bank date, transaction type), reference number and date, party GSTIN, place of supply, the e-invoice
IRN and acknowledgement, the e-way bill number.
FinCom's accuracy checks: an entry is held only when its lines do not total zero (held with plain words, nothing of it
applied). Any other mismatch (items' taxable value plus tax against the ledger lines, e.g. GST on freight with no item
line, round-off, a discount; bill-wise or cost centres against their line) enters the entry as Tally has it, and Sync
activity says "Entered in the books; to check: ..." with the note in plain words.
One entry per request, by Tally's own id (FinComVoucherByMaster) or by type and number (FinComVoucherByNumber);
read only, inside the 2-second rule, after postings. One limit: a very large invoice (over about 1 MB of details,
roughly 1,000 items or more) is held with plain words for a Day Book upload.
With it (part B, masters) a ledger created or altered in Tally reaches FinCom within about 10 minutes, and a ledger an
entry uses that FinCom does not have is fetched from Tally first, then the entry (FinComLedgerChanges,
FinComLedgerByName; read only, inside the 2-second rule, after postings).
Needs FinCom's cloud update of 2.3.1 and database migrations 56, 57, 58, 59 and 60 (in that order) on staging before you
start.

The app is not updated with this bridge. Keep the add-on loaded as it is
(C:\ProgramData\FinCom\addon\FinComRecorder.tdl). 2.3.1 does not change the add-on.

Before you start: you are signed in to FinCom as the owner, TallyPrime 7.1 is open with the add-on loaded, and
GARG SHEKHAR is open in Tally. In every entry you make for this sheet, start the narration with TRIAL. Use stock items
and ledgers that already exist in GARG SHEKHAR (any items; a sales ledger and a purchase ledger with GST; the CGST and
SGST ledgers).

Write down the time (IST) of each check you do; we need it if something fails.


INSTALL (over 2.3.0, for your own Windows user)
   New versions install by themselves on every computer. To install it at once: open the Link, double-click the setup;
   if Windows warns: More info > Run anyway; "Just for me" (as before); Next.
   You should see: "FinCom Bridge 2.3.1 is installed and running". Within a minute, FinCom's Tally page shows version
   2.3.1 for your line ("NWS144 · <your Windows user>").
   Every Windows user on NWS144 that runs a FinCom Bridge gets 2.3.1 the same way.


CHECK 1 - A SALES INVOICE WITH TWO ITEMS AND GST
   In Tally make a Sales entry in item invoice mode: a party, TWO stock items (for example 2 of one item and 3 of
   another), the sales ledger with GST, and CGST and SGST on the invoice. Narration "TRIAL 231 sales". Save it. Note its
   number, the invoice total, the taxable value and the CGST and SGST amounts Tally shows.
   You should see: within a minute, Sync activity shows it as Created, and it is in the books (not held). Look up shows
   every line: the party for the invoice total, the sales ledger for the taxable value (the two items together), CGST
   and SGST for Tally's amounts. The lines add up to the total, as in Tally.
   If not, send: its type and number, the time you saved it, a picture of the entry in Tally, what Sync activity shows
   for it, and the Show log lines starting "Recorder:" that name it.

CHECK 2 - A PURCHASE INVOICE WITH ITEMS
   In Tally make a Purchase entry in item invoice mode: a supplier, one or two stock items, the purchase ledger with
   GST, CGST and SGST (input). Narration "TRIAL 231 purchase". Save it. Note its number and Tally's figures.
   You should see: within a minute, Sync activity shows it as Created, and it is in the books with every line: the
   supplier for the total, the purchase ledger for the taxable value, CGST and SGST input for Tally's amounts.
   If not, send: its type and number, the time, a picture of the entry in Tally, and the Show log lines starting
   "Recorder:" that name it.

CHECK 3 - A CREDIT NOTE WITH ITEMS
   In Tally make a Credit Note in item invoice mode against the party of CHECK 1: one of the items returned, the sales
   ledger, CGST and SGST. Narration "TRIAL 231 credit note". Save it. Note its number and Tally's figures.
   You should see: within a minute, Sync activity shows it as Created, and it is in the books with every line: the
   party, the sales ledger, CGST and SGST, matching Tally.
   If not, send: its type and number, the time, a picture of the entry in Tally, and the Show log lines starting
   "Recorder:" that name it.

CHECK 4 - ALTER THE SALES INVOICE
   Open the Sales entry of CHECK 1 in Tally, change the quantity of one item, and save.
   You should see: within a minute, Sync activity shows it as Altered, and the books show Tally's new figures for every
   line (party, sales ledger, CGST, SGST).
   If not, send: its type and number, the time, and the Show log lines starting "Recorder:" that name it.

CHECK 5 - ITEM INVOICES HELD UNDER 2.3.0 SETTLE BY THEMSELVES
   Only if an item invoice was held under 2.3.0 with the words "the entry's details from Tally are incomplete (its
   lines do not add up ...)". Wait up to 15 minutes after installing 2.3.1, with that company open in Tally.
   This includes an item invoice 2.3.0 had already asked Tally for again (held a second time with the same words): it
   is asked once more and settles the same way (needs FinCom's cloud update of 2.3.1).
   You should see: the held entry is in the books with every line, once, and its held lines show as 'replaced'.
   If not, send: its type, number and date, and the Show log lines starting "Recorder:" that name it.

CHECK 6 - A RECEIPT AGAINST A BILL
   Make one Receipt of Rs 1 from the party of CHECK 1, against that sales invoice's bill (Agst Ref), narration
   "TRIAL 231 receipt". Save it.
   You should see: within a minute, Sync activity shows it as Created, and it is in the books with its bill-wise
   detail (the bill's name, Agst Ref, Rs 1).
   If not, send: its number, the time, and the Show log lines starting "Recorder:" that name it.

CHECK 7 - THE SALES INVOICE'S ITEMS, E-INVOICE AND E-WAY BILL
   Look at the Sales entry of CHECK 1 in FinCom.
   You should see: each item with its quantity, unit, rate, taxable value, HSN and GST rate as on the invoice in Tally,
   and CGST and SGST per item adding up to the invoice's CGST and SGST. If the invoice has an e-invoice IRN or an e-way
   bill number in Tally, FinCom shows the same.
   If not, send: a picture of the invoice in Tally (with its e-invoice details) and what FinCom shows.

CHECK 8 - A BANK PAYMENT WITH A UTR
   Make a Payment from a bank ledger of Rs 1, transaction type e-Fund Transfer (or NEFT), with a UTR / instrument number
   and date, narration "TRIAL 231 bank". Save it.
   You should see: within a minute, in the books with the UTR, the instrument date and the transaction type.
   If not, send: its number, the time, a picture of its bank details in Tally, and the Show log lines.

CHECK 9 - A PAYMENT WITH TDS
   Make a Payment (or Journal) with TDS deducted on a party with a TDS nature of payment, narration "TRIAL 231 tds".
   Save it.
   You should see: within a minute, in the books with its TDS details: nature of payment, section, rate, amount, and
   the party's deductee type (once the ledger list of 2.3.1 has come). Note what Tally shows as the section; if FinCom
   shows the section blank, send a picture of the entry's TDS details in Tally (the section field is not yet seen from a
   real Tally).
   If not, send: its number, the time, a picture of the entry in Tally, and the Show log lines.

CHECK 10 - A JOURNAL WITH COST CENTRES
   Make a Journal of Rs 2 split between two cost centres (Rs 1 each), narration "TRIAL 231 cost centres". Save it.
   You should see: within a minute, in the books with its two cost centre allocations (category, centre, amount).
   If not, send: its number, the time, a picture of the entry in Tally, and the Show log lines.

CHECK 11 - A LEDGER CHANGED IN TALLY REACHES FINCOM (MASTERS)
   In Tally, alter one party ledger: change its GSTIN (or its state, or its opening balance). Save it.
   You should see: within about 10 minutes, FinCom's ledger list shows the new GSTIN (state, opening) for that ledger.
   The log has a line "Ledger changes for <company>: ... 1 ledger(s) sent to FinCom".
   If not, send: the ledger's name, the time, and the Show log lines starting "Ledger changes".

CHECK 12 - A NEW PARTY USED AT ONCE: THE LEDGER FIRST, THEN THE ENTRY
   Make a Sales entry of Rs 1, narration "TRIAL 231 new party". In the party field press Alt+C and create a new party
   "TRIAL 231 PARTY" (Sundry Debtors). Save the entry.
   You should see: for a moment Sync activity may show it held "waiting for the ledger 'TRIAL 231 PARTY' from Tally";
   within a few minutes the party is in FinCom's ledger list and the entry is in the books, once.
   The log has a line "Ledger TRIAL 231 PARTY of <company>: fetched from Tally before its entry".
   If not, send: the entry's number, the time, and the Show log lines starting "Ledger" and "Recorder:".

CHECK 13 - A LEDGER RENAMED IN TALLY, USED UNDER ITS NEW NAME
   In Tally, rename one party ledger that FinCom already has (for example "TRIAL 231 PARTY" to "TRIAL 231 PARTY NEW").
   At once, make a Sales entry of Rs 1 to it under the new name, narration "TRIAL 231 renamed". Save it. Then make a
   second one the same way, narration "TRIAL 231 renamed 2".
   You should see: within a few minutes both entries are in the books under FinCom's ledger "TRIAL 231 PARTY" (FinCom
   keeps the old name in 2.3.1), once each; no second ledger with the new name; the second entry goes in without waiting.
   If not, send: the entries' numbers, the time, and the Show log lines starting "Ledger" and "Recorder:".

CHECK 14 - TALLY SLOW FOR A WHILE: NOTHING SWITCHED OFF, IT TRIES AGAIN BY ITSELF
   In Tally on NWS144, open a large report (for example Display More Reports > Day Book for the whole year) so that
   Tally is busy for a few minutes. Meanwhile make a Journal entry of Rs 1 in GARG SHEKHAR, narration "TRIAL 231
   retry", from another Tally window if you have one, else right after the report finishes.
   You should see: FinCom's Tally page shows for your line "Tally did not answer in time at <time>; trying again by
   itself at <time>", with no Resume button; once Tally is free again (within 5 minutes) the line goes back to
   "Reading" by itself and the entry is in the books. Nothing on the page says "switched off" or "Stopped by itself".
   The log has one line per try ("did not answer in time ... trying again by itself at") and one line "answered in time
   again".
   The held Journal lines from 2.3.0 (GARG SHEKHAR, MasterIDs 25689 and up) come into the books within a few minutes
   of 2.3.1 starting, without anyone pressing anything.
   If not, send: the time, a photo of the Tally page line, and the Show log lines starting "Tally" and "Recorder:".

How to send the log lines: right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > Show log. Each line starting "Recorder:" says
what the bridge decided for one entry: asking Tally, taken, held (with the reason), or not asked (with the reason).
On NWS144 each Windows user has their own FinCom icon and their own log: send the log of the Windows user named in the
check.


HOW TO ROLL BACK (go back to 2.3.0)
   1. Right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > "Roll back to the previous version".
   2. A question names the version it goes back to (2.3.0). Answer Yes.
   3. The icon goes away for a few seconds and comes back.
   You should see: within a minute, FinCom's Tally page shows version 2.3.0 for your line. The app does not change:
   it trusts 2.3.0 as before. Item invoices are then held again as under 2.3.0 (uploading that day's Day Book settles
   them). Automatic updates stay off on this computer until FinCom turns them on again.
