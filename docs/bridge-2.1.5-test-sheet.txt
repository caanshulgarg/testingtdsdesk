What is new in 2.1.5 (over the 2.1.4 build that was never handed out)
  - Tally's per-ledger voucher list ("Vouchers : Ledger") is never asked: Tally builds that list ledger by ledger, the
    same shape of read that hung Tally on 02-Oct-2026 (ledgers 696-699).
  - After a posting, and when a posting is resumed or retried, the entries are looked for by their FinCom id alone
    (FinComTag: one date's entries, heads and narration only, one request per date).
  - FinCom's "a ledger's entries" (/ledgervouchers, /ledgerlines) is answered from the copy kept on this computer; for
    a period the copy does not cover it says the entries are shown from FinCom's copy. Tally is not asked.

What is new in 2.1.4 (since 2.1.3)
  - Tally is never asked for a balance: no opening balances read a batch at a time (TDSDeskKeepBal), no trial balance,
    no ledger balance, no Ledger Vouchers report. FinCom works balances out from its copy of the books.
  - Until the change read is measured, Tally is read only by Update now and the nightly run: the day book of the year,
    a month a request (a month that does not answer in 25 s is read again in halves, down to a day), each month saved as
    it comes. Opening a client in FinCom, or a posting, reads nothing from Tally.
  - The plain ledger and group lists come back (02-Oct-2026): Update now (and the nightly run) also reads every
    ledger's name, group, stored opening (the master's own field, not a balance), GSTIN, PAN, GUID, MasterID and AlterID,
    2,000 ledgers a request by MasterID (a chunk that does not answer in 20 s is read again in halves, from where it
    stopped), and the groups in one request. The list is also read once after a posting that made a new ledger, and when
    FinCom's ledger chooser asks for it (at most once per company every 3 minutes). A ledger renamed in Tally is renamed
    in FinCom (the old name kept); one deleted in Tally is marked deleted in FinCom, never removed.
  - One request to Tally at a time; a posting goes before anything else waiting. After a request Tally did not answer,
    nothing is sent except one small check (the company's name and GUID), at most once a minute, until Tally answers it.
  - The company's GUID is held: a company of the same name with another GUID (restored, re-created) is neither read nor
    posted to.
  - Every voucher posted carries its FinCom id at the end of its narration ("TDSDesk:<id>"); before posting, Tally is
    looked through for that id on the voucher's date first, then the party / bill / date / amount check of 2.1.4.
  - A posting sent when Tally stopped answering shows "Checking whether it reached Tally" and is not sent again until
    Tally answers and the id has been looked for: found -> In Tally; not found -> sent once more.
  - Only one bridge reads or posts a company at a time (a lease in FinCom's cloud, once migration-32 is applied).
  - Measure Tally (for FinCom support), in the tray and as a command: see docs/tally-measure-sheet.txt.

Use ZZ TEST for every posting below, never GARG SHEKHAR & COMPANY.

1. INSTALL OVER 2.1.3 (OR 2.1.4)
   Do: TallyPrime open with ZZ TEST. Open the Link, then in PowerShell:
       Get-FileHash $env:USERPROFILE\Downloads\FinComBridge-Setup-2.1.5.exe
       Double-click the setup; "Windows protected your PC" > More info > Run anyway; "Just for me"; Next.
   You should see: the Fingerprint above. "FinCom Bridge 2.1.5 is installed and running", "Main bridge: reading and
       posting". You are not asked to pair again. Right-click the icon > Status shows version 2.1.5; the menu has
       "Measure Tally (for FinCom support)".
   If not: the page's title and reason; keep "Send install log to FinCom" ticked and click Finish.

2. 30 IDLE MINUTES: NO REQUEST
   Do: right after installing, note icon > Status "Requests sent to Tally since the bridge started: N". Leave Tally and
       FinCom open, touch nothing for 30 minutes (opening a client in FinCom is fine).
   You should see: the count of requests to Tally the same after 30 minutes. In Show log no "Tally 9000:" line and no
       "Keeping ..." line in those minutes; opening a client logs "Light update ...: Tally is not read".
   If not: the log of those minutes.

3. UPDATE NOW READS THE LEDGER LIST AND THE DAY BOOK, NOTHING ELSE
   Do: in FinCom open ZZ TEST and press Update now. Watch Show log.
   You should see: first "Keeping ZZ TEST: ledger list MasterID 1-2000, N ledger(s) (x.xs); saved" (one line per 2,000
       MasterIDs, the last one "...-end"), then "Keeping ZZ TEST: the ledger list read (N ledgers, G groups, R request(s)):
       ... new, ... changed, ... renamed, ... deleted". Then "Keeping ZZ TEST: day book 20260401-20260430, N entries (x.xs),
       M day(s) changed; saved", one line a month up to today, then "the day book from ... to ... read (N entries); M day(s) changed since the last read".
       No "KeepBal", "opening balances", "balance" or "trial balance" line anywhere in the log. Press Update now again:
       the same months, "0 day(s) changed".
   If not: the log from "Update from Tally: asked for now".

4. A POSTING DURING A READ GOES AT ONCE (TIMED IN SECONDS)
   Do: press Update now on GARG SHEKHAR & COMPANY (a long read), and while its "day book ..." lines appear, post one
       approved ZZ TEST bill from FinCom. Start a stopwatch (or note the clock to the second) the moment you press Post.
       Stop it when FinCom shows the bill "In Tally (verified)".
   Write down: the seconds from Post to "In Tally (verified)": ____ s. From Show log, the time of "Posting job ..." and
       of "Posting job ... done" (to the second).
   PASS if: the bill is "In Tally (verified)" within 10 seconds; the log says "a background read was stopped at once so
       FinCom's request goes first", then the read goes on from the month it was on. In Show log no line between the
       posting's start and "done" says "waited ...s for another FinCom request".
   FAIL if: more than 10 seconds, or "busy with another FinCom request", or the read starts again from April.
   If not: what FinCom showed, the seconds you timed, and the log of that minute.

5. A POSTING WITH TALLY SHOWING A MESSAGE BOX
   Do: in TallyPrime press Alt+F4 so the "Quit?" box shows; leave it. In FinCom post another approved ZZ TEST bill.
   You should see: either "Could not check Tally, not posted. Try again." (the check was not answered; nothing sent), or,
       if the bill had already been sent when Tally stopped answering, "Checking whether it reached Tally" on the Post page
       (the entry's state "unknown"). The log: "Tally 9000 is busy (no answer in 25s); nothing more is sent to it until it
       answers a small check" and then at most one "FinComCompany" check a minute.
   Then: close the box with No. Within a minute or two: "Tally 9000 answered the small check"; the bill becomes
       "In Tally (verified)" (it had arrived: "Created (Tally answered late)") or is sent once more and verified. In
       TallyPrime's Day Book: the bill ONCE.
   If not: what FinCom showed, the Day Book of that date, and the log of those minutes.

6. THE SAME BILL AGAIN (THE FINCOM ID)
   Do: after step 4, press Post again on the same bill in a second browser tab that still shows it as not posted.
   You should see: "Already in Tally (voucher no. X, dd-mm-yyyy)"; the log: "duplicate check ...: NOT POSTED, its FinCom
       id is in Tally already". In TallyPrime the voucher's narration ends with "TDSDesk:<id>". Still ONE voucher.
   If not: a screenshot of both tabs, the Day Book of that date, and the log.

7. MEASURE TALLY
   Do: icon > Measure Tally (for FinCom support) with GARG SHEKHAR & COMPANY open; wait (a few minutes).
   You should see: a report opens (measure-...txt in the bridge's folder) with lines a, b, c1, c2, d, e (and its fields),
       f0 and the ledgers 696-699, each with ms, bytes and count; a ledger that does not answer in 25 s says HANGS, and
       the bridge waits for the small check before the next one.
   Then: follow docs/tally-measure-sheet.txt (parts A and B) and send the report and the filled tables.

8. THE LEDGER LIST: A NEW, A RENAMED AND A DELETED LEDGER
   Do: in TallyPrime (ZZ TEST) create a ledger "ZZ Ledger Test 1" under Sundry Creditors, with an opening of 100 Cr and,
       if you like, a GSTIN. In FinCom press Update now on ZZ TEST.
   You should see: the log "the ledger list read (...): 1 new, 0 changed, 0 renamed, 0 deleted" and "Cloud: ZZ TEST: the
       ledger list sent (1 added, ...)". In FinCom, ZZ TEST's ledger chooser (a bill's ledger, or Look up) lists
       "ZZ Ledger Test 1" under Sundry Creditors; its opening is 100 Cr.
   Do: in TallyPrime rename it to "ZZ Ledger Test 2" (Alter the ledger, change the name). Press Update now again.
   You should see: the log "Keeping ZZ TEST: ledger ZZ Ledger Test 1 is now ZZ Ledger Test 2 in Tally" and "... 1
       renamed ...". In FinCom only "ZZ Ledger Test 2" is listed (not both), with the same group and opening.
   Do: in TallyPrime delete "ZZ Ledger Test 2" (it has no entries, so Tally allows it). Press Update now again.
   You should see: the log "ledger ZZ Ledger Test 2 is no longer in Tally; it is marked deleted in FinCom (never
       removed)" and "... 1 deleted". On the cloud ZZ TEST's row for it is still there, with deleted_at set (FinCom's
       pages show it as deleted, or leave it out of the chooser, once the app reads deleted_at). If the log says "not marked in FinCom yet (its cloud cannot mark
       deletions until migration-32 is applied)", migration-32 is not on staging yet: note it, that is expected.
   Also: a posting from FinCom that makes a new ledger is followed, a few seconds after it finishes, by "Ledger list of
       ZZ TEST (after a posting with a new ledger)" and the new ledger is in the list without pressing Update now.
   If not: the log from "Update from Tally: asked for now", and what FinCom showed.

9. PAUSE BACKGROUND READING
   Do: right-click the tray icon > Pause background reading. Right-click > Status.
   You should see: Status says "Background reading paused: opening a client in FinCom, the ledger chooser's refresh, the
       ledger list after a posting and the nightly catch-up do not read Tally. Postings and Update now (with the ledger
       list) still work." While paused: open a client in FinCom, open a bill's ledger chooser: no "Keeping ..." line in
       the log, the requests count in Status does not move. Post a ZZ TEST bill: it posts as usual. Press Update now: it
       reads the ledger list and the day book as in step 3.
   Then: right-click > Resume background reading (the item has that name while paused).
   If not: Status (the text) and the log of those minutes.

10. A FULL WORKING DAY (ACCEPTANCE)
   Do: leave 2.1.5 running on NWS144 from the start to the end of a normal working day, with TallyPrime used as usual by
       the office. During the day post at least three approved ZZ TEST bills from FinCom, at least one of them while
       Update now is reading (as in step 4). At the end of the day: icon > Show log, and icon > Status.
   PASS only if all four hold:
       a) Tally stayed usable all day: no one in the office saw Tally freeze, "Not Responding", or a wait on saving that
          they had to report; the log has no "Tally 9000 is busy (no answer in ...)" line caused by the bridge's own read.
       b) Three postings, each "In Tally (verified)", each ONCE in TallyPrime's Day Book.
       c) The posting during Update now confirmed within seconds (10 s at most, timed as in step 4).
       d) The longest request of the day is 20 s or less: in Show log no "Tally 9000: ... took N.Ns" line with N above
          20 (lines are written for every request of 3 s or more).
   Write down: the start and end time of the day; the three postings' times; the seconds of the posting during Update
       now; the longest "took" line of the day.
   If not: which of a-d failed, the time, and the log of that hour. These go into docs/bridge-2.1.5-acceptance.md.

11. CLEAN UP
   Do: in TallyPrime delete the ZZ TEST vouchers made in steps 4-6 and 10 (or Remove from Tally in FinCom), and the test
       ledger of step 8 if it is still there.

   d) icon > Status (the text).
