What 2.3.4 changes (the full notes: docs/bridge-2.3.4-notes.md; the exact request: docs/fast-request-form.md):
1. One entry is asked of Tally by its MasterID with Tally's keyed object export (FinComVoucherObject): milliseconds,
   whatever the company's size (GARG SHEKHAR's 2.2 s, VMS EVENTS' larger company the same). The old request is gone.
2. Exactly the approved fields are kept; everything else Tally sends is dropped before anything is logged or sent.
3. No company should be marked "no longer asked"; a company 2.3.3 marked is asked again.
4. Held lines 2.3.3 ended with the Day Book words are asked once more (FinCom lists them for 30 days).
5. "Test fetching an entry" has forms B, D, E and F only.
FinCom's cloud: tally-ingest is deployed with this release (no migration). The add-on is unchanged.

Before you start: you are signed in to FinCom as the owner, TallyPrime 7.1 is open on NWS144 with the add-on loaded,
GARG SHEKHAR & COMPANY, IX DESIGNS and VMS EVENTS open in the Tally of the Windows user whose bridge you check. In every
entry you make for this sheet, start the narration with TRIAL. Write down the time (IST) of each check.

CHECK 1 - THE VERSION
   Install FinComBridge-Setup-2.3.4.exe and compare its fingerprint with the one above.
   You should see: the tray says FinCom Bridge 2.3.4; FinCom's Tally page shows version 2.3.4 for your line. The log
   says the entry fetch of GARG SHEKHAR & COMPANY (marked by 2.3.3) is lifted.

CHECK 2 - A SAVE IN THE LARGE COMPANY ENTERS AT ONCE
   In GARG SHEKHAR make a Journal of Rs 1, narration "TRIAL 234 big 1". Open FinCom's Sync activity for it.
   You should see: within a few seconds the entry is in the books with its details (not held). Repeat in VMS EVENTS.
   If not, send: the time, a photo of Sync activity, and the Show log lines starting "Recorder:".

CHECK 3 - NO COMPANY MARKED
   Over an hour of normal work, NWS144's card on the Tally page never says "FinCom has stopped asking Tally for this
   company's entries".

CHECK 4 - THE HELD LINES OF 2.3.3 SETTLE
   Within half an hour of installing, GARG SHEKHAR's, IX DESIGNS' and VMS EVENTS' held lines (the 29 Journals of 06-Oct
   among them) become "Replaced by a later line" and their entries are in the books once. A line Tally still does not
   give says "... upload that day's Day Book to settle it": upload that day's Day Book.

CHECK 5 - NOTHING SILENT
   Over the day, every journal you saved shows in Sync activity: in the books, or held with a reason. None is missing.

How to send the log lines: right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > Show log. On NWS144 each Windows user has their
own FinCom icon and their own log: send the log of the Windows user named in the check.

HOW TO ROLL BACK (go back to 2.3.3)
   1. Right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > "Roll back to the previous version".
   2. A question names the version it goes back to (2.3.3). Answer Yes.
   3. The icon goes away for a few seconds and comes back.
   You should see: within a minute, FinCom's Tally page shows version 2.3.3 for your line. Automatic updates stay off on
   this computer until FinCom turns them on again.
