## Checked, no finding

- **Migration 73 is add-only.** It adds 5 columns (`if not exists`) and one table, and makes no deletes. It is one transaction with `lock_timeout 10s`. Its `tally_ingest_details` is exactly 72's text plus the one `perform tally_ingest_tdstally` line; I diffed the two function bodies. Both new functions are `security definer` with `search_path = public, pg_temp`. `tally_ingest_tdstally` is executable by nobody, and `tally_ingest_natures` by service_role only. `tally_tds_natures` has RLS on, a select-only policy `firm_id = my_firm()` (the same pattern as the other tally_* tables), select for authenticated only, and `firm_id` taken from `tally_books`. The `alter` value is checked against `^[0-9]{1,18}$` before the cast, and jsonb sizes are capped (tdsx 200 kB per voucher; natures 2,000 × 20 rows × 2 kB). I found no SQL injection path.
- **The XML filters** go through the existing `fcCollection` / `masterRange` helpers, and the company is escaped as before. `natListID` is in `allowlist.go`, `pinned.go` (pinMasterRange) and `requestClass` (undated).
- **Natures.** They are read inside the ledger round, through the one gate, under the round's budget and `keepHold()`. A failure never holds the ledger list. After a no-answer, the gate itself marks Tally busy and refuses further sends until the small check answers (seen in the test log), so a second request does not pile onto a busy Tally.
- **Upgrade from 2.4.2.** `ledFromArr` reads absent columns as "". `diffLedgers` compares whole rows, so ledgers with TDS data are re-sent once after the upgrade. `pushLedgerList` adds the TDS columns only to 13-element outbox rows; 2.4.2 rows go out with 11 columns, and the cloud then leaves the columns as they are (`ledTds` needs 14 or more). A half-read `ledgers-read` file from 2.4.2 corrects itself on the next round. Kept voucher files are untouched (prospective only).
- **The fastvch blank-drop** keeps a non-TDS entry's stripped text identical to 2.4.2's.
- **The app** shows "From Tally" marks and a "Tally and FinCom differ" panel, and never changes a FinCom tag (`:183-189`). `TDSCH.tag` refuses any batch over the challan's balance. A certificate FinCom holds under the same number is kept. A person's section (`byHand` / `ok`) is kept. `tdsxOf` uses `tds_tally` only at the entry's own AlterID, so a stale challan from an older version is ignored. I saw no AI and no new network destinations.

Verdict: 4 High, 3 Medium, 6 Low.
