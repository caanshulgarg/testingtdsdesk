What 2.4.7 changes (the owner's "yes" of 24-Oct-2026: close the pairing gap of the 2.4.6 security review; nothing new is
asked of Tally; FinCom's page is updated with it and must be the review build of the same day or later):
P1. Only FinCom's own pages may talk to the bridge: "null" (any website's sandboxed frame) and http://localhost are no
    longer trusted, even if an older settings file names them. Developers and FinCom's tests switch them on with
    "DevOrigins": true in the settings (off by default).
P2. A typed code is checked without ever giving away anything that lets the code be worked out: the bridge no longer
    answers with a proof made from the 6 digits. FinCom and the bridge check the code together (SPAKE2); the 6 digits
    never leave the page, each check is one of the five tries for that code, a wrong code says so.
P3. FinCom opened by the bridge (the tray's Connect FinCom on this computer..., the installer's last page, the first
    start) carries the code and a long secret of that connect window (#pair=123456-<32 letters and digits>); FinCom
    pairs with the secret once the bridge proved it holds it. The message box still shows the 6 digits only.
P4. The tray sends the bridge's key only to the program on the bridge's port that proved it holds that key (a program of
    another Windows user that took the port while the bridge was down gets nothing).

   run out ... again".

   computer's connect code already filled in (the address ends with #pair=, which FinCom takes off at once), and the
   computer connects by itself. After installing you only press "Add as client" on the Tally page.

   Untick "Open FinCom to connect" and it does not open by itself. A silent install (/S) never opens FinCom.
D. The code works as before: once, for 10 minutes. Only FinCom's own https addresses are opened; the address carries
   only the 6-digit code, never the bridge's key.

What 2.4.5 changed (kept: the full notes, docs/bridge-2.4.5-notes.md):
1. Older entries from Tally only for a period you choose: Books > From Tally (and Connect Tally after step 6), "Bring older
   entries from Tally": a financial year, Whole year / Apr-Sep / Oct-Mar (the halves once the year passes 20,000
   vouchers), Start, Pause. The bridge reads one day at a time, only while nobody works in Tally; your entries go first.
   Progress reads like "8,412 of 12,960 vouchers (65%) - 4,548 left".
2. A day Tally cannot give within 2 seconds even one voucher type at a time shows one quiet line: "Tally took too long to
   give <day>. Please upload that day's Day Book." with an Upload button. Nothing else about Tally is shown.
3. Connect Tally step 5 shows the exact ledger count: "312 of 1,240 ledgers".
4. The installer adds FinCom's add-on to each TallyPrime's tally.ini (TDL = C:\ProgramData\FinCom\addon\FinComRecorder.tdl,
   and User TDL = Yes only if there is no User TDL line), keeping a backup tally.ini.fincom-bak. A tally.ini with
   "User TDL = No" is left as it is and shown under Help & support.
5. Job progress files are written so that no update is lost.
6. FinCom's cloud stores these (migration 98, run on staging before the tally-ingest of 2.4.5).
7. Night and Sunday pace: outside office hours (20:00-08:00 IST and all Sunday unless you change them on FinCom's Tally
   page, per computer) the chosen period is read day after day while Tally is idle; the progress line shows the time
   left ("about 1 h 40 min left at night pace"). For a big company's first load, the Day Book export (Alt+E) and upload
   is still the quick way: Books > From Tally says so.
8. Payroll in the older entries: a payroll voucher of the period comes with every employee's pay heads (its entry
   request may take Tally up to 3 seconds, as in 2.4.4); the payroll's employees are read first; a salary payment keeps
   the employee and amount, never the bank details.

Before you start: migration 98 has run on staging and the tally-ingest of 2.4.5 is deployed; you are signed in to FinCom
as the owner; TallyPrime 7.1 is open on NWS144 with a ZZ TEST company that has entries in an earlier year. Copy
C:\Program Files\TallyPrime\tally.ini somewhere before installing. In every entry you make for this sheet, start the
narration with TRIAL. Write down the time (IST) of each check.

CHECK 1 - THE VERSION AND TALLY.INI
   Close TallyPrime. Install FinComBridge-Setup-2.4.7.exe and compare its fingerprint. Open tally.ini in Notepad.
   You should see: the tray says FinCom Bridge 2.4.7; FinCom's Tally page shows version 2.4.7; tally.ini has the line
   TDL = C:\ProgramData\FinCom\addon\FinComRecorder.tdl after your other TDL lines (they are all still there, in the
   same order); tally.ini.fincom-bak next to it is your old file. Open TallyPrime: F1 > TDLs & Add-ons lists FinCom's
   add-on and every add-on you had.

CHECK 2 - THE LEDGER COUNT
   Connect Tally > step 5 > read the ledgers again.
   You should see: "N of M ledgers" counting up, M the exact number of ledgers in Tally (Gateway > Display More Reports >
   List of Accounts shows the same count).

CHECK 2b - TEST READING OLD DAYS (read only; do this BEFORE CHECK 3)
   Right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > "Test reading old days (read-only)". It suggests the smallest company open
   in Tally; answer Yes for a small company (No suggests the next one). Leave Tally alone for a minute.
   You should see: a message "Old-days test: 0.21 s / 0.34 s / 0.18 s - no freeze" (three times, each under 2 s), and the
   same line on FinCom's Tally page under this computer (Help & support > Details). Nothing changes in Tally or in FinCom.
   If a time says "longer than 2 s" or "did not answer": stop here and send the line to FinCom; do not do CHECK 3.

CHECK 3 - OLDER ENTRIES
   Only after CHECK 2b showed "no freeze": on FinCom's Tally page, under this computer's Details, "Reading older entries on
   this computer: off" > Switch on (owner only).
   Books > From Tally > Bring older entries from Tally: choose last year, Whole year, Start. Leave Tally alone.
   You should see: "Counting the year in Tally... X of 365 days", then "X of Y vouchers (P%) - Z left" going up, Tally
   never freezing; at the end "all in FinCom". Y equals Tally's voucher count for that year (Day Book, Ctrl+F12 period).
   If Tally is ever slow for a day (over 2 s), FinCom stops reading by itself and shows one line asking for the Day Book of
   the days still missing; it does not try again until you choose the period again.

CHECK 3b - NIGHT PACE AND PAYROLL (after 20:00 IST, or on a Sunday)
   FinCom's Tally page > this computer's Details: "Faster reading of older entries outside office hours: 20:00 to 08:00
   and all Sunday (India time)" > Change lets you set other hours (owner only). With CHECK 3 still going, after 20:00:
   You should see: the progress line goes up faster and ends with "about ... left at night pace"; Tally never freezes.
   If the company has payroll in that year: Payroll > the payroll voucher of a month in the period shows in FinCom's
   books with each employee's pay heads; the salary payment shows each employee's amount, and no bank account number.

CHECK 3c - TALLY NEVER HELD BY THE CHANGE LIST (2.4.5 ROUND 104; a company of more than 5,000 vouchers)

   You should see: within a minute, FinCom's Tally page shows version 2.4.6 for your line. 2.4.6 pairs with the older
   proof again (the pairing made with 2.4.7 stays). Automatic updates stay off on this computer until FinCom turns them
   on again.
