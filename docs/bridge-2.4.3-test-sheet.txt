What 2.4.3 changes (the full notes: docs/bridge-2.4.3-notes.md):
1. A TDS payment's challan as Tally keeps it reaches FinCom: BSR code, challan number and date (with the cheque, bank
   and branch), and what each line of the payment pays (tax, interest, late fee). FinCom makes the challan from it,
   marked "From Tally", with the interest and the fee in their own columns of the FVU file.
2. The deductions Tally says a payment pays are tagged to that payment's challan in FinCom ("From Tally"). A deduction
   FinCom already has on another challan is never moved: the difference is shown on the Challans tab.
3. From Tally's masters: a TDS ledger's nature of payment (its section is taken from it, before the one in the ledger's
   name), a party's lower-deduction certificate (it appears in FinCom's certificate list, marked "From Tally"), and each
   nature's thresholds (the limits tracker uses them).
4. The bridge asks Tally a little more with its ledger list (the ledgers' TDS settings) and the natures of payment once a
   round (a few dozen masters); one request at a time, never while a posting runs. Entries are asked exactly as in 2.4.2.
5. FinCom's cloud stores these (migration 73, run on staging before the tally-ingest of 2.4.3).
The add-on is 2.4.2's. The two data locations (one company in two places) move to 2.4.4.

WHAT YOUR STAFF DO IN TALLY (so the challan reaches FinCom)
   a. Pay the TDS with Tally's Stat Payment: Payment voucher (F5), the bank, then Ctrl+F (Autofill) > Stat Payment,
      Type of duty/tax TDS, the period, the nature (or all), Cash/Bank: Tally fills the TDS lines and links them to the
      deductions they pay. Accept.
   b. After the bank's challan: Display More Reports > Statutory Reports > TDS Reports > Challan Reconciliation, the
      period, select the payment (Space), then Reconcile: type the BSR code, the challan number and the challan date
      (and the cheque or DD number and date, the bank). Accept. (The owner checked on a licensed Tally that these stay
      saved; the test machines run Tally's Educational mode, where the keys did not save them.)
   c. Interest or late fee paid with the tax: give each its own line in the Stat Payment with Type of payment Interest or
      Late Fee (Tally's own field), not added into the tax line.
   d. A lower-deduction certificate: on the party ledger, the TDS details' lower deduction (certificate number, section
      197, from, to, rate, limit) for the nature of payment it covers.

Before you start: migration 73 has run on staging and the tally-ingest of 2.4.3 is deployed; you are signed in to FinCom as
the owner; TallyPrime 7.1 is open on NWS144 with GARG SHEKHAR & COMPANY. In every entry you make for this sheet, start the
narration with TRIAL. Write down the time (IST) of each check.

CHECK 1 - THE VERSION
   Install FinComBridge-Setup-2.4.3.exe and compare its fingerprint. The add-on is unchanged: nothing to reload.
   You should see: the tray says FinCom Bridge 2.4.3; FinCom's Tally page shows version 2.4.3.

CHECK 2 - THE FIRST REAL CHALLAN (the live confirmation)
   Do a. and b. above for the next real TDS payment (or a TRIAL one of Rs 1, narration "TRIAL 243 challan").
   You should see: on the TDS page, Challans tab, a challan with that BSR code, number and date, marked "From Tally"; the
   deductions the payment pays tagged to it ("From Tally" beside their TDS). If the payment had an Interest or Late Fee
   line, the challan's interest and fee show them. Write down the challan's BSR code and number: this is the first real
   Challan Reconciliation entry and confirms that Tally's TAX* tags carry it.

CHECK 3 - A NATURE OF PAYMENT AND A CERTIFICATE
   Press Update now on the Tally page (the ledger list is read again).
   You should see: on the Certificates tab, any lower-deduction certificate the parties carry in Tally, marked "From
   Tally"; a certificate already typed in FinCom stays as typed, and where Tally says otherwise the difference is listed.

CHECK 4 - NOTHING SLOWER
   Save an ordinary Journal of Rs 1, narration "TRIAL 243 speed".
   You should see: it is in FinCom's books as fast as with 2.4.2 (a few seconds); the log shows the entry request
   answered in well under 2 seconds, and after Update now a line "natures of payment ... (0.0s)".

How to send the log lines: right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > Show log.

HOW TO ROLL BACK (go back to 2.4.2)
   1. Right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > "Roll back to the previous version".
   2. A question names the version it goes back to (2.4.2). Answer Yes.
   3. The icon goes away for a few seconds and comes back.
   You should see: within a minute, FinCom's Tally page shows version 2.4.2 for your line. Entries 2.4.2 sends carry no
   challan details; the ones 2.4.3 sent keep theirs. Automatic updates stay off on this computer until FinCom turns them on again.
