FINCOM RECORDER TRIAL  -  instruction sheet (bridge 2.1.10)
=====================================================

What this is
------------
A small Tally add-on that writes down every save, change, cancel, delete and import that Tally
reports for the company that is open in Tally. It shows nothing on screen, adds no menus or keys
and never stops a save.
In this sheet, "the company you test on" is the one company you choose for the trial: the
company you test on (it must be linked to a FinCom client).
IMPORTANT: since bridge 2.1.10 the add-on does not check the company's name: while it is loaded
it writes for WHICHEVER company is open in this Tally. So: only the company you test on is open
in Tally while the add-on is loaded. Unload it (PART 4) as soon as the script is done, and before
you work in any other company.
You only use Tally's own menus, FinCom and the FinCom tray icon. You do not open or edit any file.

PLEASE READ - what the trial leaves in the company you test on
----------------------------------------------------------------
The trial adds test entries to the company you test on: about 1,200 (PART 3B) + 100 (PART 3C)
+ the entries you make in the script (PART 2 and 3C), all marked TRIAL (narration starting
TRIAL; the bench ledgers are named TRIAL Bench Dr and TRIAL Bench Cr). They are real entries in
that company's books. They reach FinCom's copy of the client (and its figures: TDS, ledgers,
reports) once the recorder is live or a Day Book of that company is uploaded. Cancelling them
in Tally afterwards is the owner's choice.
Everything in FinCom or the tray that adds entries asks first, naming the company:
"Make N test copies of this bill" and the REMOTEID second send (Post page), and "Recorder trial:
time saving" (tray). Each adds TRIAL entries only after you answer Yes.

Before you start
----------------
0. FinCom > Tally page > this computer: switch "Trial tools on this computer" ON (the owner
   only; it is off for every computer until you switch it on). Within half a minute the FinCom
   tray icon shows the trial items ("Test reading from Tally" and the four "Recorder trial: ..."
   items). Switch it off again when the trial is over: the items disappear.
- Bridge 2.1.10 must be installed on this PC (its installer puts the add-on at
  C:\ProgramData\FinCom\addon\FinComRecorderTrial.tdl).
- Leave the antivirus exactly as it is (real-time scanning ON). We want the real delay.
- Close every company except the company you test on before starting. Open only that company,
  and keep only that company open until PART 4 (unload) is done.
- In every entry you make by hand in this trial, type TRIAL at the start of every narration
  (for example "TRIAL payment 1"), so each test entry can be found later.


PART 1  -  LOAD THE ADD-ON IN TALLYPRIME
----------------------------------------
1. In TallyPrime press F1 (Help) at the top right.
2. Choose  TDLs & Add-Ons.
   (If you do not see it: press Alt+G (Go To), type  TDL  and choose  TDLs & Add-Ons.)
3. Press F4  (Manage Local TDLs)  -  shown at the right side of the screen.
4. Set  "Load selected TDL files on startup"  to  Yes.
5. In  "List of TDL files to preload on startup"  go to the first empty line and type exactly:
       C:\ProgramData\FinCom\addon\FinComRecorderTrial.tdl
   Press Enter, then accept the screen (Ctrl+A, or answer Yes to "Accept?").
6. Back on the TDLs & Add-Ons screen, the file is listed. Its status must say  Loaded.
   - If it says anything else (for example an error), stop here and use the tray:
     "Recorder trial: send results". That sends the error to us. Then do PART 4 to unload it.

Names in other TallyPrime versions (same screen, slightly different words):
   - TallyPrime 1.x / 2.x:  F1: Help > TDLs & Add-Ons > F4: Manage Local TDLs
   - TallyPrime 3.x / 4.x / 5.x:  F1: Help > TDLs & Add-Ons > F4: Manage Local TDLs
     (the first line may read "Load TDL files on startup" instead of
      "Load selected TDL files on startup" - it is the same Yes/No.)
   - Older Tally.ERP 9 (not expected on NWS144): Gateway > F12: Configure > Product & Features
     > F4: Manage Local TDLs.


PART 2  -  THE TEST, ON THE COMPANY YOU TEST ON, IN THIS ORDER
----------------------------------------------
Do one step at a time. Wait a few seconds between steps. Type TRIAL at the start of every
narration you type.

 1. Tray icon > "Recorder trial: note change numbers".
 2. Create one voucher (for example a Payment of Rs 1 from Cash to any expense). Save it.
    Narration: "TRIAL payment 1". Then open the Day Book and check that the voucher appears ONCE (not twice).
 3. Open that same voucher again, change the narration (keep TRIAL at its start, for example "TRIAL payment 1 changed"),
    save it.
 4. Tray icon > "Recorder trial: note change numbers".
 5. Cancel a voucher: open a TRIAL voucher you made, press Alt+X (Cancel Vch), answer Yes.
 6. Tray icon > "Recorder trial: note change numbers".
 7. Delete a voucher: open a TRIAL voucher you made, press Alt+D (Delete), answer Yes.
 8. Tray icon > "Recorder trial: note change numbers".
 9. Import one voucher: from FinCom, on the client linked to the company you test on, use
    "Make N test copies of this bill" with N = 1 (it asks first, naming the company; the copy's
    narration starts TRIAL) and post it (this reaches Tally as an import through Tally's port,
    the same way all FinCom postings arrive).
10. Ledgers, one after the other:
      a. create a new ledger  "TRIAL Recorder Test"  (any group), save;
      b. open it again, change anything (for example the address), save;
      c. open it again, rename it to  "TRIAL Recorder Test 2", save;
      d. open it again and delete it (Alt+D, Yes).
11. Tray icon > "Recorder trial: send results".

If Tally shows ANY message you have not seen before during these steps, write down the words
on paper, finish the steps you can, and still do step 11.

PART 3B  -  BULK POSTING WITH THE ADD-ON (owner, 04-Oct-2026), on the company you test on
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why: FinCom will post up to 2,000 entries at a time. This measures what the add-on costs a bulk
posting, and whether Tally tells the add-on about entries that arrive through its port.

Before you start
 - Only the company you test on open in Tally. Posting from FinCom to its client only.
 - On FinCom's Tally page, this computer's Posting settings, "bills per request": set 50 (the trial uses 50 per
   request, as a bulk posting would). Put it back to 10 afterwards.
 - Pick one approved bill of that client. On the Post page use "Make N test copies of this
   bill". It asks first: "This will add N test entries to <company>. Continue?" - check it names
   the company you test on, then Yes. Each copy's narration starts "TRIAL |".

Round A - add-on LOADED (as after PART 1)
 A1. Tray icon > "Recorder trial: note change numbers".
 A2. Make 100 test copies. Post them (Post on the Ready list). Wait until the page says
     "Posted 100 of 100". Do nothing else in Tally meanwhile.
 A3. Tray icon > "Recorder trial: note change numbers".
 A4. Make 500 test copies. Post them. Wait for "Posted 500 of 500".
 A5. Tray icon > "Recorder trial: note change numbers".
 A6. Tray icon > "Recorder trial: send results".

Round B - add-on OFF
 B1. Do PART 4 (unload the add-on). Check it no longer shows as Loaded.
 B2. Repeat A1 to A5 (100, then 500) exactly as above.
 B3. Tray icon > "Recorder trial: send results".

Afterwards: the company you test on has about 1,200 more test entries, all marked TRIAL (see
"PLEASE READ" at the top). Cancelling them in Tally afterwards is the owner's choice.

What we report back to you from the two sends (nothing else is needed from you):
 - the import time of each posting (100 and 500, with and without the add-on), from the bridge's
   own timing of every request;
 - the delay the add-on adds per entry = (time with - time without) / number of entries;
 - how many lines the add-on wrote in each posting, and of which events;
 - whether the import events fire for entries that arrive through Tally's port: if the file has no
   import line for a posted entry, they do not, and the design's case "imports not seen" applies;
 - the change numbers before and after each posting (from the "note change numbers" lines), so the
   rise in Tally's change number per posting is known.
Tally's own clock in the add-on counts whole seconds only, so the per-entry delay comes from the
bridge's request timings (milliseconds), not from the add-on's lines.


PART 3C  -  CAN THE ADD-ON HANG OR SLOW TALLY (owner, 04-Oct-2026), on the company you test on
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why: to see, on your own Tally, whether the add-on ever makes a save wait, and whether turning it
off is instant and safe. Only the company you test on is touched. Three short tests, in this
order.

Before you start
 - Only the company you test on open in Tally. Bridge 2.1.10 running (FinCom icon near the
   clock), "Trial tools on this computer" on (step 0).
 - The company you test on stays linked to its FinCom client. The 100 journals of test 1 (time
   saving) are test entries in the company you test on (narration "TRIAL FinCom bench <n>", no
   FinCom tag). They reach FinCom's copy once the recorder is live or a Day Book of that company is
   uploaded. Afterwards the client may show "up to 100 changes not received": that is these
   journals and is expected.

Test 1 - the time a save takes, with and without the add-on
 1a. The add-on loaded (as after PART 1).
 1b. Tray icon > "Recorder trial: time saving". It asks first: "This will add 100 test entries
     (and 2 ledgers if missing) to <company>. Continue?" - check it names the company you test
     on, then Yes. It posts 100 test journals there (narration "TRIAL FinCom bench <n>"; it makes
     two ledgers, "TRIAL Bench Dr" under Indirect Expenses and "TRIAL Bench Cr" under Sundry
     Creditors, the first time), one at a time, and times each save. Wait for its box (a few
     minutes). Write down the two lines it shows (milliseconds per save).
 1c. Do PART 4 (unload the add-on). Check it no longer shows as Loaded.
 1d. Tray icon > "Recorder trial: time saving" again: Yes. Write down the two lines.
 1e. Load the add-on again (PART 1) for test 2.

Test 2 - the add-on's file held by another program
 2a. The add-on loaded. Save one voucher (narration starting TRIAL) in the company you test on
     first if you have not yet today (so its file exists).
 2b. Tray icon > "Recorder trial: lock the holding file for 30 s". A box says the file is locked for
     30 seconds from now. Close the box.
 2c. Within those 30 seconds: in the company you test on create a Payment of Rs 1 (narration
     "TRIAL lock test") and save it (Ctrl+A).
     Write down: did the save wait? Did any message appear (its words)? Did the entry save?
 2d. After the 30 seconds: Tray icon > "Recorder trial: send results".

Test 3 - the off switch, without closing Tally
 3a. Do PART 4 (unload the add-on) WITHOUT closing Tally. Write down the time.
 3b. In the company you test on save one voucher (a Payment of Rs 1, narration "TRIAL off
     test").
 3c. From FinCom, on its client: "Make N test copies of this bill" with N = 1 (Yes to the
     question naming the company), and post it. It should say posted.
 3d. Tray icon > "Recorder trial: send results".
 We check that no line was written after the time in 3a, and that the posting went through.

What to send us: the lines you wrote down in tests 1 and 2, the time from 3a, and the tray
messages after each "send results". The test journals stay in the company you test on
(narration "TRIAL FinCom bench"); cancelling them in Tally afterwards is the owner's choice.


PART 4  -  UNLOAD THE ADD-ON (when the script is done, before you work in any other company,
           or if PART 1 showed an error)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. F1 (Help) > TDLs & Add-Ons > F4 (Manage Local TDLs).
2. Either: go to the line  C:\ProgramData\FinCom\addon\FinComRecorderTrial.tdl  and clear it
   (remove the line: Alt+R or blank it out), or set "Load selected TDL files on startup" to No
   (this turns off every TDL in the list - use it only if this was the only one).
3. Accept the screen. The file should no longer show as Loaded.
   If it still shows, close TallyPrime and open it again.


What to send us
---------------
Only the tray message that appears after "Recorder trial: send results", plus the words of any
Tally message you wrote down. Nothing else. When the trial is over, switch "Trial tools on this
computer" off again on FinCom's Tally page.
