FinCom Bridge: fix in place, the real-Tally test (phase 0), for the owner
==========================================================================

What this checks: the two Tally requests the owner approved on 23-Nov-2026 (findings/fix-in-place-plan.md section 2.3),
FinComFixVoucher and FinComFixLedger, on a real Tally, before the bridge release that carries them. Branch
next-bridge-fix-alter (it goes into the release after 2.5.20). Use the ZZ TEST company only (made-up entries), and
ZZ BIG TEST for the times. Do every step on TallyPrime 3.0, 4.1, 5.1, 6.2 and 7.1 (the tally-versions run can do 1-8
by itself; the owner's own run on NWS144 is 7.1).

Before you start
  - ZZ TEST open in Tally, FinCom Bridge (test build of next-bridge-fix-alter) running, FinCom open on staging with
    ZZ TEST's client linked to the company.
  - In ZZ TEST make three sales vouchers (accounting invoice, item invoice, one with bill-wise details and an IRN typed
    in), each with a number longer than 16 characters, e.g. ZZ/2026-27/000001, and one party ledger "ZZ Buyer" with no
    GSTIN. Note each voucher's lines, bill-wise details, narration and IRN (take a screenshot of each in Tally).

1. Invoice number (FinComFixVoucher)
   FinCom > GST > the month > What to fix > Invoice number too long > Open the entry. Type ZZ/26-27/0001. Save in Tally.
   Expect: "Saved in Tally and FinCom Books." in under 2 s; the row leaves the list.
   In Tally: the voucher's number is ZZ/26-27/0001; EVERY ledger line, item line (quantity, rate, godown, batch), the
   bill-wise details, the narration and the IRN are exactly as in your screenshot. Do this for all three vouchers.
   If any line or detail is lost on any Tally version: stop, note the version, do not release (the plan's A2 round trip
   is then needed instead, a new approval).

2. A voucher type numbered automatically
   Set the Sales voucher type's numbering to Automatic, then repeat step 1 on a fourth voucher.
   Expect either "Saved in Tally ..." or "Tally kept its own value. This is how the entry is now." Note which, per
   version.

3. Place of supply (FinComFixVoucher)
   A sale with no place of supply (Party details > Place of supply blank) > Open the entry > choose Maharashtra > Save.
   Expect: Saved; in Tally the voucher's Party details > Place of Supply is Maharashtra; nothing else changed.

4. Party GSTIN on the ledger (FinComFixLedger)
   An invoice to ZZ Buyer in "Buyer GSTIN missing" > Open the entry > type the made-up GSTIN 27AAPFU0939F1ZV > Save.
   Expect: Saved. In Tally: Alter > Ledger > ZZ Buyer > GST registration details: GSTIN 27AAPFU0939F1ZV, registration
   type Regular, state Maharashtra, the date it applies from unchanged (or the books' first day if there was none);
   the ledger's name, group, opening balance and bank details unchanged.
   Note: the invoice itself keeps the GSTIN Tally stored on it when it was entered (decision 5: the ledger only). Note
   on each version whether the What to fix row leaves the list (it does only when the invoice has no GSTIN of its own).

5. PAN on the ledger (FinComFixLedger)
   Not yet on a screen: skip until the TDS screens offer it.

6. Changed in Tally first (the AlterID check)
   Open an entry's window in FinCom. Without saving, alter the same voucher in Tally (change its narration) and save it
   there. Now press Save in Tally in FinCom.
   Expect: "Changed in Tally since it was opened here. This is how it is now." Nothing changed in Tally (your narration
   change stays; the number is NOT changed). The window shows the entry as Tally has it now.

7. Tally refuses
   Type a number that another voucher of the same type already has (with Prevent duplicates on) > Save.
   Expect: "Tally did not change it: <Tally's own words>." Nothing changed in Tally or FinCom.

8. Tally closed, and typing
   a) Close Tally. In FinCom make a change and Save. Expect "Waiting for Tally."; Changes made here says Waiting for
      Tally. Open Tally (ZZ TEST). Within a minute of FinCom's GST page being open, the change is made: Saved.
   b) Start typing a voucher in Tally (leave the screen open), then Save in FinCom. Expect "Waiting for Tally."; it goes
      once you leave the voucher screen.
   c) While FinCom posts a batch of entries, Save a change. Expect it waits and goes right after the posting.

9. Undo
   After step 3, Changes made here > Undo on the place of supply change. Expect: Saved; Tally has the earlier state.

10. Only the approved forms
    In the bridge's log (sync\bridge.log) every fix shows as FinComFixVoucher or FinComFixLedger. A posting made the
    usual way still goes (Post one sale). An Alter sent through the old posting path is refused (the automated test
    TestPlainImportRefusesAlter covers this; nothing to do by hand).

11. Times (ZZ BIG TEST, 100,000 entries, 20,000 ledgers)
    Steps 1, 3 and 4 on ZZ BIG TEST: note the time of each Tally request in the bridge's log (the Alter, and the two
    reads around it). Every one must be under 2 s, Tally's window held under 2 s. Put the worst times and the date in
    allowlist.go (FinComFixVoucher, FinComFixLedger) and docs/tally-allowlist.md before the release.

12. FinCom Books and the cloud copy
    After step 1, on another computer (no Tally) open the same client: the voucher shows the new number within a
    minute (the cloud copy got the altered entry).

Results: per version, per step: pass / fail, with the time for step 11 and a screenshot for any failure.
