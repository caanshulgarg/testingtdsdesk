What 2.4.1 changes (the full notes: docs/bridge-2.4.1-notes.md):
1. A save whose MasterID points at an older entry, or at no voucher, is found once by its type and number (Sales 297 of
   09-Oct). Another type, date or number stays held as in 2.4.0.
2. The log says why an entry was not asked by its number.
3. After a restart the bank-date route does not read and send again the entries the add-on's lines explained.
4. FinCom keeps the bridge's own time a line was read.
The two data locations (one company open in two places) moved to 2.4.2. There is no migration for 2.4.1 (no migration
to run on staging), and the add-on is 2.4.0's.

Before you start: tally-ingest of 2.4.1 is deployed; you are signed in to FinCom as the owner; TallyPrime 7.1 is open on
NWS144 with GARG SHEKHAR & COMPANY. In every entry you make for this sheet, start the narration with TRIAL. Write down the
time (IST) of each check.

CHECK 1 - THE VERSION
   Install FinComBridge-Setup-2.4.1.exe and compare its fingerprint. The add-on is unchanged: nothing to reload.
   You should see: the tray says FinCom Bridge 2.4.1; FinCom's Tally page shows version 2.4.1.

CHECK 2 - A NEW ENTRY
   In GARG SHEKHAR make a Journal of Rs 1, narration "TRIAL 241 one".
   You should see: the entry in FinCom's books within a few seconds, as in 2.4.0.

CHECK 3 - A RESTART DOES NOT SEND AGAIN
   Alter that Journal (narration "TRIAL 241 two"), wait until it is in the books, then right-click the FinCom icon >
   Exit, and start FinCom Bridge again.
   You should see: the log does NOT say "Tally's voucher counter moved with no add-on line ... each is read again from
   Tally" for it; Sync activity shows no second line for it.

CHECK 4 - AN ENTRY WHOSE MASTERID IS AN OLDER ENTRY (the 09-Oct case)
   Not made by hand: the bridge's tests cover it (fallback241_test.go). On NWS144, a line held with "Tally's voucher with
   that MasterID is not a change after the starting point" from now on should be in the books, or held with "the voucher
   with that MasterID in this Tally is an older entry, not this save; asked by its type and number: ...".

How to send the log lines: right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > Show log.

HOW TO ROLL BACK (go back to 2.4.0)
   1. Right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > "Roll back to the previous version".
   2. A question names the version it goes back to (2.4.0). Answer Yes.
   3. The icon goes away for a few seconds and comes back.
   You should see: within a minute, FinCom's Tally page shows version 2.4.0 for your line. Automatic updates stay off on
   this computer until FinCom turns them on again.
