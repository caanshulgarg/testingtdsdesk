What is new in 2.1.9 (over 2.1.8) - the owner's rule of 04-Oct-2026: reading is PROSPECTIVE ONLY
  - The bridge never reads earlier entries from Tally in normal running. Update now (from FinCom or the app) and the
    nightly catch-up ask Tally only the tiny company check (FinComCompany: the company's
    name, GUID and change numbers ALTVCHID / ALTMSTID) and the ledger list (masters, 2,000 at a time, as before). No
    Day Book, no voucher list, no day is sent to FinCom's cloud. The log says, per company and run:
      "Keeping ZZ TEST: Reading old entries is off (prospective only); FinComCompany ALTVCHID=... ALTMSTID=..."
    (the old-day reading is still in the program behind the setting ReadDays, off; FinCom's cloud cannot switch it on).
    A client opened in FinCom asks Tally nothing, as before.
  - FY 2026-27's history (the entries already in Tally) comes from your Day Book upload in FinCom, not from the bridge.
  - The starting point: the first time the bridge sees a company's change numbers it keeps them in
    sync\start-point.json (once; never moved). A company that shows another GUID (a restored copy, or another company
    of the same name) gets its own starting point beside the first; neither is ever moved.
    The heartbeat carries the starting point and the latest numbers, so FinCom's cloud can see what changed since.
  - A company seen open in Tally gets the company check once when it is first seen, then at most every 10 minutes
    while it stays open (nothing else is read). The heartbeat also says whether the recorder add-on has written for
    that company in the last 7 days (how FinCom spots a computer without the add-on).
  - Posting is unchanged.
  - Four tray items for the recorder trial: "Recorder trial: note change numbers", "Recorder trial: send results",
    "Recorder trial: lock the holding file for 30 s" and "Recorder trial: time saving (ZZ TEST)" (the last two: see
    PART 3C of the trial sheet).
  - The install makes two folders: C:\ProgramData\FinCom\recorder (Users may add files there: Tally's add-on writes
    its lines there; they cannot delete the folder) and C:\ProgramData\FinCom\addon (the trial add-on,
    FinComRecorderTrial.tdl, is put there). Anything already at those places that is not a plain folder is moved
    aside, and the install log says so.
  - "Test reading from Tally" first asks a yes/no naming the company it will read.
  - "Measure Tally (for FinCom support)" measures only its undated items while reading old entries is off; its report
    says the dated items were not run.

0. BEFORE THE INSTALL
   TallyPrime open with ZZ TEST. Nothing to press in FinCom.

1. DOWNLOAD AND INSTALL OVER 2.1.8
   Do: open the Link, check the fingerprint if you can (PowerShell: Get-FileHash <file>). Double-click the setup;
       "Windows protected your PC" > More info > Run anyway; "Just for me" (as 2.1.8); Next.
   You should see: "FinCom Bridge 2.1.9 is installed and running", "Main bridge: reading and posting".
   Check: the folders C:\ProgramData\FinCom\recorder and C:\ProgramData\FinCom\addon exist, and
          C:\ProgramData\FinCom\addon holds FinComRecorderTrial.tdl.

2. TEST READING FROM TALLY (once)
   Do: right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > "Test reading from Tally". A box asks "Test reading from Tally
       on the company <name>?": check it names ZZ TEST, then Yes (No: nothing is read). Wait for its message box (a
       few minutes at most: about a dozen small requests, one at a time).
   You should see: the company and a day (the anchor: the newest date among the entries Tally lists), then one line
       per request: the Day Book with the dates in four forms (yyyymmdd; d-MMM-yyyy; each also with TYPE="Date"),
       FinComTag, FinComCompany (change numbers), "Entries above the starting point (... no dates)", and FinComCompany
       sent as UTF-16 and as UTF-8; then the line
         "Dates on this Tally: <form> (the form that answered <day> with exactly its entries; nothing was changed)"
       or "Dates on this Tally: none".
   This test only reads and writes to the log. It changes nothing: no form is switched, nothing goes to FinCom.
   Send: "Show log" > the lines starting "Test reading from Tally:" (or "Recorder trial: send results" in step 5,
       which sends the last 500 log lines too).

3. WHAT THE TALLY PAGE IN FINCOM SHOWS
   The bridge 2.1.9. The heartbeat now carries each company's starting point, its latest change numbers and whether the
   recorder has written for it in the last 7 days (shown once FinCom's cloud shows them). No new entries appear from the bridge's reading (it reads no old days);
   entries come from your Day Book upload and from postings.

4. UPDATE NOW (check that no old entries are read)
   Do: FinCom > Tally page > Update now.
   You should see in the log: "Keeping ZZ TEST: Reading old entries is off (prospective only); FinComCompany
       ALTVCHID=... ALTMSTID=..." and the ledger list lines; no "day book" line.

5. THE RECORDER TRIAL (item 99)
   The steps in Tally (loading the add-on on ZZ TEST, the exact Tally menu words, the entries to make, delete and
   cancel): see the trial sheet, docs/recorder-trial-sheet.txt. The add-on file is
       C:\ProgramData\FinCom\addon\FinComRecorderTrial.tdl
   Around the steps of the trial sheet:
   a. Before a delete and before a cancel: tray > "Recorder trial: note change numbers". You should see a box with
      "ZZ TEST: ALTVCHID=..., ALTMSTID=..., at <time>" (added to recorder-changenumbers.txt in the bridge's own sync
      folder, which the box names; no longer in the recorder folder).
   b. After the delete and after the cancel: press it again.
   c. At the end: tray > "Recorder trial: send results". You should see "Sent to FinCom support: N lines in K files
      (reference ...)". It sends the recorder folder's .txt files (at most 50, the last 4 MB of each), the change
      numbers noted in a., Tally's tdlerror.log, tally.imp and tally.ini (read only, and only from Tally's folder under
      Program Files), the last 500 lines of the bridge's log and a summary with this computer's name and the time the bridge saw
      each write (to the millisecond). The log says "Recorder trial: N lines in K files sent".

5b. CAN THE ADD-ON HANG OR SLOW TALLY
   The three short tests of PART 3C of the trial sheet (time saving with and without the add-on, the file locked for
   30 s while you save, the add-on unloaded without closing Tally).
   ZZ TEST stays linked to its FinCom client. The 100 journals of "time saving" are test entries in ZZ TEST
   (narration "FinCom bench <n>", no FinCom tag). FinCom reads no old entries, so they reach FinCom only if a ZZ TEST
   Day Book is uploaded. Afterwards the ZZ TEST client may show "up to 100 changes not received": that is these
   journals and is expected; nothing else is needed.

6. A POSTING (unchanged)
   Do: post one bill from FinCom to ZZ TEST as in 2.1.8.
   You should see: posted by Tally's reply, as before.

If anything is not as written: tray > "Send install log to FinCom", and note the step number.
