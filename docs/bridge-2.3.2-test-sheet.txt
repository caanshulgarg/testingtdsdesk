What 2.3.2 changes (the full notes: docs/bridge-2.3.2-notes.md): two things, nothing else.
1. A held line whose entry Tally did not give in time (stopped at 2 seconds, not answered, Tally busy) is asked again
   after 1 h, then after 4 h more; if that also times out it ends, FinCom shows it held with "Tally did not answer in
   time for this entry 3 times; upload that day's Day Book to settle it", and the bridge never asks for it again.
   (2.3.1 asked again at every retry, for 7 days, each ask costing Tally about 5 seconds on a large company.)
2. A company whose entry request is stopped at 2 seconds on 2 separate occasions, while Tally answered other requests
   in time around them, is marked: the bridge no longer asks Tally for its entries. Its new entries go up held at once
   with "FinCom does not ask Tally for this company's entries: finding one entry took Tally longer than 2 s. Upload
   that day's Day Book to settle it."; its held lines end with the same words. A Tally that froze as a whole does not
   count. The mark survives a restart and lifts only when a newer FinCom Bridge is installed.
No Tally request was added or changed. The 2-second stop, the retry schedule, postings first and one request at a time
are unchanged. FinCom's cloud is unchanged (no migration). The add-on is unchanged.

The app that goes with 2.3.2 shows on the Tally page, on the computer's card, one line per company marked:
   "<company>: FinCom has stopped asking Tally for this company's entries, because finding one entry took Tally longer
   than 2 seconds (since 07-Oct 10:05). New entries wait as held lines; upload that day's Day Book to settle them. This
   lifts when a faster FinCom Bridge is installed."

Before you start: you are signed in to FinCom as the owner, TallyPrime 7.1 is open on NWS144 with the add-on loaded,
GARG SHEKHAR & COMPANY and IX DESIGNS PRIVATE LIMITED are open in the Tally of the Windows user whose bridge you check.
In every entry you make for this sheet, start the narration with TRIAL. Write down the time (IST) of each check.

CHECK 1 - THE VERSION
   Install FinComBridge-Setup-2.3.2.exe and compare its fingerprint with the one above.
   You should see: the tray says FinCom Bridge 2.3.2; FinCom's Tally page shows version 2.3.2 for your line.

CHECK 2 - A SMALL COMPANY AS BEFORE
   In GARG SHEKHAR make a Journal of Rs 1, narration "TRIAL 232 small".
   You should see: within a minute it is in the books with its details, as in 2.3.1. Nothing new on the Tally page.
   If not, send: the time and the Show log lines starting "Recorder:".

CHECK 3 - THE LARGE COMPANY IS MARKED
   In IX DESIGNS make a Journal of Rs 1, narration "TRIAL 232 large 1". Wait 5 minutes.
   You should see: on NWS144's card on the Tally page: "IX DESIGNS PRIVATE LIMITED: FinCom has stopped asking Tally for
   this company's entries, because finding one entry took Tally longer than 2 seconds (since <date time>). New entries
   wait as held lines; upload that day's Day Book to settle them. This lifts when a faster FinCom Bridge is installed."
   The log has one line "IX DESIGNS PRIVATE LIMITED: entry fetch stopped: over 2 s (...)".
   If it is not marked after 15 minutes, make one more entry there ("TRIAL 232 large 2") and wait again.
   If not, send: the time, a photo of the Tally page, and the Show log lines starting "Recorder:" and "Tally".

CHECK 4 - NO MORE ASKING FOR IT
   In IX DESIGNS make another Journal of Rs 1, narration "TRIAL 232 large 3".
   You should see: it shows in FinCom at once as held, with "FinCom does not ask Tally for this company's entries:
   finding one entry took Tally longer than 2 s. Upload that day's Day Book to settle it." Tally does not pause.
   The 9 IX DESIGNS lines held under 2.3.1 end with the same words within a few minutes.

CHECK 5 - THE DAY BOOK SETTLES THEM
   Upload IX DESIGNS' Day Book for today in FinCom.
   You should see: the held lines of today settle; the entries are in the books.

CHECK 6 - A RESTART KEEPS IT
   Right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > Exit, then start FinCom Bridge again (or restart the computer).
   You should see: the line on the Tally page stays; a new IX DESIGNS entry still goes up held at once.

CHECK 7 - (OPTIONAL) A TIMED-OUT LINE OF A SMALL COMPANY
   In Tally open a large report (Day Book for the whole year) so that Tally is busy for a few minutes, and meanwhile save
   a Journal of Rs 1 in GARG SHEKHAR, narration "TRIAL 232 timed out".
   You should see: the line is held; the bridge asks for it again after 1 h (and in 4 h more if Tally is still busy
   then); once Tally answers, it is in the books. If it times out 3 times in all, it shows held with "Tally did not
   answer in time for this entry 3 times; upload that day's Day Book to settle it", and it is never asked again.

How to send the log lines: right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > Show log. On NWS144 each Windows user has their
own FinCom icon and their own log: send the log of the Windows user named in the check.

HOW TO ROLL BACK (go back to 2.3.1)
   1. Right-click the FinCom icon near the clock > "Roll back to the previous version".
   2. A question names the version it goes back to (2.3.1). Answer Yes.
   3. The icon goes away for a few seconds and comes back.
   You should see: within a minute, FinCom's Tally page shows version 2.3.1 for your line and the line about IX DESIGNS
   goes. 2.3.1 asks Tally for IX DESIGNS' entries again (each about 5 seconds), and asks its held lines again at every
   retry as before. Automatic updates stay off on this computer until FinCom turns them on again.
