FINCOM BRIDGE 2.1.10 - TEST SHEET (NWS144, staging)

File:        FinComBridge-Setup-2.1.10.exe

(2.1.9 is installed on NWS144. Install 2.1.10 over it; the installer replaces the program and keeps the pairing, the
 settings and the copy.)

In this sheet, "the company you test on" is the one company you choose for the trial: the company you test on (it
must be linked to a FinCom client). The sheet names no company.

What is new in 2.1.10 (over 2.1.9)
  - The missing starting point (what you saw on 2.1.9: background reading Paused since 03-Oct, the company open, no
    starting point, no "changes not received" flag):
      - the light company check (FinComCompany: the company's name, GUID and change numbers) now runs while background
        reading is paused. The pause was for the old heavy reads, which are all off now; it stays as you set it.
        A stop of reading (from FinCom, or the bridge's own) still stops it, and a posting still goes first;
      - a posting that stopped part-way (interrupted) or that waits for Tally no longer holds the check back; only a
        posting actually going does;
      - the list of open companies is asked afresh (the small company-list request; for a company not seen before
        also the small company-info request, its GSTIN and PAN, once) when it is older than 10 minutes, so a company
        opened later is seen. When Tally does not give the list (busy, or a posting went first), it is asked again at
        the next turn;
      - each check writes one line in the log: "Light check of <company>: starting point recorded (...)",
        "... unchanged starting point ..." (at most once an hour per company), or "... skipped: <why>" (once per
        company and kind of reason every 10 minutes);
      - the heartbeat lists every company open in Tally (not only the kept ones) with its GUID and change numbers, in
        the shape FinCom's cloud reads.
  - Trial tools for any company (the owner's decision of 04-Oct-2026): no company-name check anywhere.
      - Owner only: FinCom's Tally page has "Trial tools on this computer" (off by default, per computer). While it is
        off the tray shows none of the five trial items ("Test reading from Tally", "Recorder trial: note change
        numbers", "Recorder trial: send results", "Recorder trial: lock the holding file for 30 s", "Recorder trial:
        time saving"), and the bridge refuses them with "Trial tools are switched off for this computer in FinCom
        (Tally page, owner)". The switch reaches the bridge within half a minute.
      - The tools work on the company open in Tally and name it. "Recorder trial: time saving" asks first: "This will
        add 100 test entries (and 2 ledgers if missing) to <company>. Continue?"; its journals' narration is
        "TRIAL FinCom bench <n>" and its two ledgers are "TRIAL Bench Dr" (Indirect Expenses) and "TRIAL Bench Cr"
        (Sundry Creditors).
      - The trial add-on writes for whichever company is open in Tally while it is loaded. It ships under a NEW file
        name, C:\ProgramData\FinCom\addon\FinComRecorderAnyCompany.tdl. The 2.1.9 file FinComRecorderTrial.tdl is
        not changed by the install (so a Tally that still loads it is not widened), but it must be unloaded (step 0).
        So: only the company you test on is open in Tally while the add-on is loaded; unload it before working in any
        other company. The bridge does not check that only one company is open: that is up to you.
      - What the add-on records: each line holds the voucher's narration, number and date, the party and ledger
        names, master names and their groups, and the Tally user name. The recorder folder
        C:\ProgramData\FinCom\recorder is readable by every Windows user on this PC.
      - "Recorder trial: send results" asks first: its yes/no names the companies whose recorder lines will be sent
        (and how many lines each) and says what a line holds; nothing is sent unless you answer Yes.
      - The trial tools turn off at once when FinCom's answer to the heartbeat does not come (FinCom not reached, the
        computer's key revoked, the cloud link turned off or changed); they come back with FinCom's next answer.
  - Posting is unchanged.

PLEASE READ - what the trial leaves in the company you test on
  The trial adds test entries to the company you test on: about 1,200 (PART 3B of the trial sheet) + 100 (PART 3C)
  + the entries you make by hand in the script, all marked TRIAL. They reach FinCom's copy of the client (and its
  figures) once the recorder is live or a Day Book of that company is uploaded. Cancelling them in Tally afterwards is
  the owner's choice. "Make N test copies of this bill", the REMOTEID second send and "time saving" add TRIAL entries
  and ask first, naming the company. In every entry you make by hand, type TRIAL at the start of every narration.
  Multi-user (a second PC on the same data, PART 3 of the trial sheet): not tested yet (the owner, 04-Oct).

0. BEFORE THE INSTALL
   Before installing 2.1.10, unload any FinComRecorderTrial.tdl (PART 4) - then load the new file by its new path
   (C:\ProgramData\FinCom\addon\FinComRecorderAnyCompany.tdl, PART 1) only when the trial starts. PART 1 and PART 4
   are in the trial sheet, docs/recorder-trial-sheet.txt. In TallyPrime: F1 (Help) > TDLs & Add-Ons > F4 (Manage Local TDLs): if FinComRecorderTrial.tdl is
   listed, clear that line and accept; it must no longer show as Loaded.
   On FinCom's Tally page, this computer (NWS144): switch "Trial tools on this computer" ON (owner only).
   TallyPrime open with the company you test on, and only that company.

1. DOWNLOAD AND INSTALL OVER 2.1.9
   Do: open the Link, check the fingerprint if you can (PowerShell: Get-FileHash <file>). Double-click the setup;
       "Windows protected your PC" > More info > Run anyway; "Just for me" (as 2.1.9); Next.
   You should see: "FinCom Bridge 2.1.10 is installed and running", "Main bridge: reading and posting".
   Check: right-click the FinCom icon near the clock: the five trial items are there (Trial tools is on). Switch
       "Trial tools on this computer" off in FinCom for a minute: the items are gone from the menu; switch it on
       again.

2. THE STARTING POINT (the fault seen on 2.1.9)
   With the company you test on open in Tally (background reading may stay Paused):
   Do: wait about a minute after the install.
   You should see: FinCom's Tally page shows a starting point for the company you test on. In "Show log":
       "Light check of <company>: starting point recorded (ALTVCHID=..., ALTMSTID=...)".
   Then: make 2 entries in Tally in that company (for example two Payments of Rs 1; narration starting TRIAL, e.g.
       "TRIAL gap 1" and "TRIAL gap 2").
   You should see: within about 10 minutes, the client in FinCom shows "up to 2 changes not received" (the bridge does
       not read old entries; FinCom sees that 2 changes happened that it has not received). The log shows a line
       "Light check of <company>: unchanged starting point ALTVCHID=...; now ALTVCHID=..., ALTMSTID=..." at most once
       an hour (the check itself still runs every 10 minutes or so).
   If nothing comes: "Show log" > the lines starting "Light check": each says what happened or why it was skipped.

3. TEST READING FROM TALLY (once)
   Do: right-click the FinCom icon > "Test reading from Tally". A box asks "Test reading from Tally on the company
       <name>?": check it names the company you test on, then Yes. Wait for its message box (a few minutes at most).
   You should see: as in 2.1.9 (the day, one line per request, then "Dates on this Tally: ...").
   This test only reads and writes to the log. It changes nothing.

4. THE RECORDER TRIAL
   The steps in Tally (loading the add-on, the exact Tally menu words, the entries to make, delete and cancel, each
   with TRIAL at the start of its narration): see the trial sheet, docs/recorder-trial-sheet.txt. The add-on file is
       C:\ProgramData\FinCom\addon\FinComRecorderAnyCompany.tdl
   (the new path; the 2.1.9 FinComRecorderTrial.tdl stays unloaded.)
   Around the steps of the trial sheet:
   a. Before a delete and before a cancel: tray > "Recorder trial: note change numbers". You should see a box with
      "<company>: ALTVCHID=..., ALTMSTID=..., at <time>".
   b. After the delete and after the cancel: press it again.
   c. At the end: tray > "Recorder trial: send results". A yes/no first names the companies whose recorder lines
      will be sent (and how many lines each) and says what a line holds (narration, number and date, party and ledger
      names, master names, Tally user name): check it names only the company you test on, then Yes. You should see
      "Sent to FinCom support: N lines in K files (reference ...)".

4b. CAN THE ADD-ON HANG OR SLOW TALLY
   The three short tests of PART 3C of the trial sheet (time saving with and without the add-on, the file locked for
   30 s while you save, the add-on unloaded without closing Tally).
   The company you test on stays linked to its FinCom client. The 100 journals of "time saving" are test entries in
   the company you test on (narration "TRIAL FinCom bench <n>", no FinCom tag). The yes/no names the company first:
   check it. Afterwards the client may show "up to 100 changes not received": that is these journals and is expected.

5. UNLOAD THE ADD-ON
   When the script is done, and before you work in any other company: PART 4 of the trial sheet. Then switch "Trial
   tools on this computer" off on FinCom's Tally page.

6. A POSTING (unchanged)
   Do: post one bill from FinCom to the client linked to the company you test on, as in 2.1.9.
   You should see: posted by Tally's reply, as before.

If anything is not as written: tray > "Send install log to FinCom", and note the step number.
