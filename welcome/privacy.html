This policy explains what personal data FinCom handles, why, who helps us handle it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. The short version

  • Two roles. For your own account we decide how your data is used. For your clients' books, bills and returns, your firm decides and we process them only on its behalf.
  • We do not sell personal data, show advertising, or use tracking or analytics cookies.
  • We do not train AI models on your data. When you use an AI feature, only what that feature needs is sent to the AI provider, and PAN, GSTIN and similar identifiers are masked before a question goes to the assistant.
  • Stored in India. Your firm's data is held in Mumbai. A few providers, such as the AI and email services, are outside India; they are listed in section 6.
  • Tally stays on your computer. The FinCom Bridge reads Tally on your computer and sends FinCom only the companies you connect.
  • You stay in control. You can see, correct, export and erase your data, and complain to our Grievance Officer or the Data Protection Board of India.

1. Who we are and our role

FinCom is online software for chartered accountant firms, tax professionals and businesses in India. It reads bills and bank statements, keeps a copy of Tally books in step, works out TDS and GST, prepares returns and reports, and posts entries back to Tally at your instruction.

Your account (we are the Data Fiduciary). When you or your firm's people register and use FinCom, we decide why and how that account data is processed. For this data we are the Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act and the body corporate under the SPDI Rules.

Your clients' data (your firm is the Data Fiduciary; we are its Data Processor). The books, bills, bank statements, returns, payroll and other records your firm uploads or connects contain personal data of your clients and of their customers, suppliers and employees. Your firm decides why that data is processed. We process it only on your firm's behalf and on its instructions, under our data processing terms. If you are a client of a firm that uses FinCom, please contact that firm first about your data; we will help it respond.

2. Data we collect

Data you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, firm name, role in the firm (owner, staff or view-only), and phone number if you give one. Your password is stored only as a one-way hash by our sign-in provider. If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, we receive your name and email address from them.
  • Two-step sign-in: if you turn it on, a secret shared with your authenticator app, held by our sign-in provider.
  • Support tickets: what you write to us and any files you attach.
  • Payments: the amounts you add to your credit, dates, GST invoice details and your firm's GSTIN. We do not collect card or bank login details; payments are made by transfer to our bank account.

Data your firm processes in FinCom (on your firm's behalf)

  • Books from Tally: through the FinCom Bridge or a Day Book file you upload: companies, ledgers, vouchers, parties with their GSTIN, PAN and addresses, balances, and earlier versions of entries that changed.
  • Bills and bank statements: the files you upload and the details read from them, such as invoice numbers, amounts, party names, GSTIN, PAN and bank transactions.
  • GST and TDS data: returns, GSTR-2B and other data fetched from the GST portal at your request, challans, deductees and their PAN, certificates and notices. If you choose to save a client's GST portal or e-invoice login, the password is kept encrypted in a secrets vault and is never shown again.
  • Payroll: for the Tally companies that use payroll, each employee's name, PAN, designation, dates of joining and leaving, and salary paid. Employees' bank account numbers, IFSC codes and bank names are removed before anything is stored. FinCom does not read employees' date of birth or Aadhaar from Tally.
  • Zoho Books: only if your firm connects a client's Zoho Books organisation, the same kinds of records from Zoho.
  • Calls, captions and voice typing: if you use calls inside FinCom, the call's participants (members, and guests by name and email), chat messages and files shared in the call. A recording, its transcript and summary are made only when recording is allowed for that client (it is off unless the firm's owner turns it on) and after every participant has been shown the recording notice. Live captions and voice typing send short audio clips for speech-to-text; captions are kept only while the call is being recorded.
  • Questions to the FinCom Assistant: the question, the files you attach, and a log of each question (its text, the tools used, tokens and cost). Answers may be cached so the same question on the same books is not sent to the AI again.

Data collected automatically

  • Usage and security records: sign-in times, the audit trail of actions such as posting to Tally, deleting entries and changes to people and credit, and counts of AI and reading use for billing.
  • Device and network: your IP address and browser details in our providers' server logs. For limits against abuse we keep only a hash of the IP address, not the address itself.
  • The FinCom Bridge: its version, the connection steps it reports, and the Tally companies it is linked to. Crash reports are sent only if you turn them on in the Bridge's settings.
  • Error reports: on our test site only, error reports with personal details scrubbed out (see Sentry).

3. How we use it

  • To provide FinCom: read documents, keep your books in step, work out tax, prepare returns, reports and letters, fetch data from government portals at your request, post entries to Tally, and run calls and the assistant.
  • To keep accounts secure: check sign-ins, prevent abuse and bots, investigate incidents, and keep an audit trail.
  • To bill you: work out monthly fees and per-use charges and issue GST invoices.
  • To support you: answer tickets and tell you about changes to the service or these terms.
  • To keep FinCom working well: find and fix faults, using scrubbed error reports and usage counts.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as tax, company law and CERT-In reporting, and lawful requests from authorities.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or build profiles of your clients. We do not send marketing messages without your consent.

4. Legal basis and consent

Account data. We process it on the basis of the consent you give when you register, through this notice, and for the legitimate uses the DPDP Act allows, such as meeting a legal obligation or responding to a medical emergency or legal process. You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to the Grievance Officer; we then stop processing, except where the law requires us to keep data, and the account closes.

Client data. Your firm, as Data Fiduciary, is responsible for having a lawful basis, including any notice and consent its clients and their employees need, before uploading or connecting their data. Our terms of service say more.

Sensitive personal data. Under the SPDI Rules, passwords and financial information such as bank statements are sensitive personal data or information. We collect them only for the purposes above, with your consent, and protect them as described in section 9.

5. AI processing

Some FinCom features use artificial intelligence. The firm's owner can switch the assistant off for the whole firm, and can keep chosen clients away from AI help in Settings.

  • Reading bills. Bills are first read by free text recognition. A bill image is sent to Anthropic's Claude or to Google Cloud Vision only when your firm's reading settings use them.
  • The FinCom Assistant and AI help. Questions about a client's books, suggestions for ledgers the rules could not settle, pairing GST invoices, drafting replies to notices, and call summaries are handled by Anthropic's Claude. Before a question goes to Claude, PAN, GSTIN, TAN, Aadhaar and phone numbers, and the names of the client's parties, are replaced by tokens, and put back only inside FinCom. A notice you attach is sent as it is. The assistant may search the web and read public court and tax department pages to check the cases it cites.
  • Speech. Voice typing and live captions send short audio clips to Groq's speech-to-text service. Captions you ask to see in another language are translated by Groq.

No training on your data. FinCom does not use your data or your clients' data to train AI models. Our AI providers process it through their business APIs, whose terms say that data sent through them is not used to train their models. AI can be wrong. AI output in FinCom is a suggestion for a professional to check. It is not a decision about you, and nothing is filed, paid or posted to Tally by AI alone.

7. Transfers outside India

Your firm's data is stored in India. Some providers in section 6 process data outside India, mainly in the United States: the AI, speech, email and error-report services, and Cloudflare's global network. We send them only what the feature you use needs. Under section 16 of the DPDP Act, we do not transfer personal data to any country the Government of India restricts by notification, and we require each provider to protect the data with security at least as strong as this policy.

8. How long we keep it

  • While your account is open, we keep your firm's data so that FinCom can work. Items you delete go to "Deleted", from where they can be restored; earlier versions of Tally entries that changed are kept with the books so that the history is complete.
  • Backups: a nightly copy of each firm's data, keeping the latest 14, and our database provider's daily backups.
  • Call recordings and transcripts: 90 days for a client's call and one year for a class, unless the firm's owner sets a different period (between 7 days and 10 years). After that the recording is marked deleted and is no longer available in FinCom. [OWNER TO CONFIRM: when marked recordings are removed from storage]

9. Security

  • Each firm walled off. Row-level security in the database refuses any request for another firm's rows, whatever the screen asks for.
  • Encryption. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Portal passwords and API keys are kept in an encrypted vault and are never sent to your browser.
  • Sign-in. Passwords of at least 10 characters, checked against known leaks; limits on repeated attempts; optional two-step sign-in with an authenticator app; automatic sign-out after a period without use.
  • Roles. The firm's owner adds and removes people and decides who may change data and who may only look.
  • The FinCom Bridge. It listens only on your own computer, answers only FinCom with a paired key, and never opens Tally to the internet.
  • Audit trail. Sign-ins, posting to or deleting from Tally, and changes to people and credit are recorded in a trail that cannot be edited.
  • Standards. We follow reasonable security practices under the SPDI Rules and are working toward ISO/IEC 27001 certification; we are not yet certified. See our security page.

If something goes wrong. If a personal data breach affects your data, we will tell the affected firm without delay, report to the Data Protection Board of India and to CERT-In within the times the law sets, and tell affected people as the DPDP Rules require.

10. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act and the SPDI Rules you have the right to:

  • Access: a summary of the personal data we process about you and how we process it, and who we have shared it with;
  • Correction and completion: have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected or completed, and data updated;
  • Erasure: have data erased when it is no longer needed for the purpose, unless the law requires us to keep it;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it; this does not affect processing before withdrawal;
  • Grievance redressal: complain to our Grievance Officer, and if you are not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India;
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights for you in the event of your death or incapacity.

Much of this you can do yourself: people and firm details in Settings, and exports from each page. Otherwise write to [CONTACT EMAIL FOR PRIVACY REQUESTS]. We may need to confirm who you are before acting. For data in a firm's books, please ask that firm first; we help it respond. When you exercise these rights, please also meet your duties under section 15 of the DPDP Act, such as not filing false or frivolous complaints.

11. Children

FinCom is for firms, professionals and businesses. You must be 18 or older to have an account, and we do not knowingly collect personal data of children for our own purposes. A firm's records may include data of a minor, for example a minor's income clubbed with a parent's; the firm is responsible for the verifiable consent of the parent or guardian that the DPDP Act requires.

12. Cookies and browser storage

FinCom does not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies, and the website has no third-party analytics. FinCom itself sets no cookies; Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary cookie for its security checks.

The app uses your browser's own storage (local storage and IndexedDB) to keep you signed in, to remember settings such as your choices on screen, and to keep a working copy of your firm's data so that work continues when the internet drops. This storage stays in your browser and is not read by anyone else. On a shared computer, sign out and clear the browser's data for FinCom.
