FinCom GST helper

No return chosen yet. In FinCom, open the client's GSTR-1, IFF or GSTR-3B, go to its Files tab and press “Open the portal for this return”.

What the helper never does
  • Type the captcha or an OTP, or read them.
  • Press File, Submit, EVC, DSC, Make payment or Post credit to ledger. Filing is always your own click.
  • Keep your portal password: it is typed into the login box once and dropped.
  • Guess a field: if one is not where it expects, it stops and says which.
