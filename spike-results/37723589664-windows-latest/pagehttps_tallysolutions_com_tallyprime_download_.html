What’s new in
TallyPrime?

Docs by Ira

Turn invoices automatically into TallyPrime entries with the power of AI by simply scanning or uploading them. Reduce manual effort, improve accuracy, and save up to 80% of processing time.
Learn More

New invoice templates & personalisation

Personalize invoices with your logo, watermark, address, terms & conditions, and more, tailored to your business needs. Access new pre-defined invoice templates with flexible customization options.

Schedule III Division I

Generate compliant financial statements for companies with net worth below Rs 250 Cr using an integrated Excel-based tool simplifying Schedule III Division I reporting.
Learn More

Connected banking and payments

Connected banking and payments now extends to ICICI Bank. Now make direct payments, real-time balances, and instant reconciliations, all within TallyPrime. Continues to support Axis Bank, State Bank of India, and Kotak Mahindra Bank.

HSN/SAC Validation

Ensure accuracy with online validation of HSN/SAC codes directly within TallyPrime, helping you stay GST compliant.

Direct Import of Amazon Reports

Are you an ecommerce seller on Amazon? Now directly import excel reports into TallyPrime to automatically record transactions.
